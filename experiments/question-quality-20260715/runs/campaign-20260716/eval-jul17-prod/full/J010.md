## 원지문 (변형 전)
There is a tendency, once the dust of an emergency has settled down, to seek the reduction of famine vulnerability primarily in enhanced economic growth, or the revival of the rural economy, or the diversification of economic activities. The potential contribution of greater economic success, if it involves vulnerable groups, cannot be denied. At the same time, it is important to recognize that, no matter how fast they grow, countries where a large part of the population derive their livelihood from uncertain sources cannot hope to prevent famines without specialized entitlement protection mechanisms involving direct public intervention. Rapid growth of the economy in Botswana, or of the agricultural sector in Kenya, or of food production in Zimbabwe, explains at best only a small part of their success in preventing recurrent threats of famine. The real achievements of these countries lie in having provided direct public support to their populations in times of crisis.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
There is a tendency, once the dust of an emergency __(A) has settled down__, to seek the reduction of famine vulnerability primarily in enhanced economic growth, or the revival of the rural economy, or the diversification of economic activities. The potential contribution of greater economic success, if it involves vulnerable groups, cannot __(B) deny__. At the same time, it is important to recognize that, no matter how fast they grow, countries __(C) where__ a large part of the population derive their livelihood from uncertain sources cannot hope to prevent famines without specialized entitlement protection mechanisms __(D) involving__ direct public intervention. Rapid growth of the economy in Botswana, or of the agricultural sector in Kenya, or of food production in Zimbabwe, explains at best only a small part of their success in preventing recurrent threats of famine. The real achievements of these countries lie in __(E) having provided__ direct public support to their populations in times of crisis.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) has settled down
(B) deny
(C) where
(D) involving
(E) having provided

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
이 문장의 주어는 'The potential contribution of greater economic success'로 사물인 '잠재적 기여'를 뜻합니다. 타동사인 deny는 '~을 부인하다'라는 뜻이므로 주어와의 관계가 수동이며, 조동사 cannot 뒤에 목적어가 수반되지 않은 구조입니다. 따라서 능동 표현인 'deny'는 이 자리에 올 수 없으며, 수동태인 'be denied'로 고쳐야 합니다. 주어와 동사 사이에 길게 삽입된 양보의 조건절 'if it involves vulnerable groups'에 이끌려 주어와 술어의 수동 관계를 놓치지 않도록 주의해야 합니다.
