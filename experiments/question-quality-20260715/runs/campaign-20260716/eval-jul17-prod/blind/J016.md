## 지문
There are interesting trade-offs in the relative importance of subject matter (i.e., figure) and style (i.e., background). In __(A) highly__ representational paintings, plays, or stories, the focus is on subject matter __(B) that__ resembles everyday life and the role of background style is to facilitate the construction of mental models. Feelings of pleasure and uncertainty __(C) carry__ the viewer along to the conclusion of the piece. In highly expressionist works, novel stylistic devices work in an inharmonious manner against the subject matter thereby __(D) created__ a disquieting atmosphere. Thus, when the work is less "readable" (or easily interpreted), its departure from conventional forms reminds the viewer or reader that an "aesthetic attitude" __(E) is needed__ to appreciate the whole episode. This active involvement provides a basis for depth of aesthetic processing and reflection on the meaning of the work. An ability to switch between the "pragmatic attitude" of everyday life and an "aesthetic attitude" is fundamental to a balanced life.
