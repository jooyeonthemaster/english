## 원지문 (변형 전)
The idea of cutting out a budget category probably makes you nervous. But being mindful about your spending doesn't have to be an all-or-nothing game. Instead of forcing yourself to go entirely without, reduce spending on that category by just 10 percent each month. You won't see sudden, drastic change in your budget, but the shift will be a lot easier to stomach. Say you spent $148 at coffee shops last month. The idea of never buying coffee is enough to make you want to hide from your budget forever, but what if you challenged yourself to spend just 10 percent less on coffee this month? That's $14.80 less, for a total of $133.20. Then, next month, see if you can bring that expense down by 10 percent again to $119.88. By introducing incremental shifts instead of huge, radical changes, you're more likely to stick with your new, moderately reduced habits.

## 단락 (순서 배열 대상)
(A) Say you spent $148 at coffee shops last month. The idea of never buying coffee is enough to make you want to hide from your budget forever, but what if you challenged yourself to spend just 10 percent less on coffee this month?

(B) Instead of forcing yourself to go entirely without, reduce spending on that category by just 10 percent each month. You won't see sudden, drastic change in your budget, but the shift will be a lot easier to stomach.

(C) That's $14.80 less, for a total of $133.20. Then, next month, see if you can bring that expense down by 10 percent again to $119.88. By introducing incremental shifts instead of huge, radical changes, you're more likely to stick with your new, moderately reduced habits.

## 발문
주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것은?

## 선택지

2 (B)-(A)-(C)
3 (B)-(C)-(A)
4 (C)-(A)-(B)
5 (C)-(B)-(A)

## 선언된 정답
2

## 해설
주어진 글에서는 예산을 대폭 삭감하는 것에 대한 불안감을 언급하며, 지출 관리가 전부가 아니면 전무인 게임이 될 필요는 없다고 밝히고 있습니다. 
따라서 지출을 완전히 끊어버리는 대신 특정 카테고리의 지출을 매달 10%씩 줄여나가라는 대안을 제시하는 (B)가 가장 먼저 와야 합니다. 
그 다음으로 그러한 10% 감축법의 구체적인 예시로 지난달 커피숍 지출 액수($148)를 예로 드는 (A)가 이어집니다. 
마지막으로 (A)에서 제안한 '10% 덜 쓰기'의 구체적 산출 결과인 $14.80 절약을 'That'으로 받아 추가적인 단계별 감축 경로($119.88)와 점진적 변화의 효과를 일반화하는 (C)가 오는 것이 자연스럽습니다. 
그러므로 가장 적절한 글의 순서는 (B)-(A)-(C)입니다.
