## 원지문 (변형 전)
Recording an interview is easier and more thorough, and can be less unnerving to an interviewee than seeing someone scribbling in a notebook. But using a recorder has some disadvantages and is not always the best solution. If the interview lasts a while, listening to it again to select the quotes you wish to use can be time-consuming, especially if you are working to a tight deadline. It is often more efficient to develop the technique (using a recorder as backup if you wish) of selective note-taking. This involves writing down the key answers from an interview so that they can be transcribed easily afterwards. It is sensible to take down more than you think you'll need, but try to get into the habit of editing out the material you are not going to need as the interview proceeds. It makes the material much easier and quicker to handle afterwards.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Recording an interview is easier and more thorough, and can be less unnerving to an interviewee than __(A) seeing__ someone scribbling in a notebook. But using a recorder has some disadvantages and __(B) is__ not always the best solution. If the interview lasts a while, listening to it again to select the quotes you wish to use can be time-consuming, especially if you are working to a tight deadline. It is often more efficient __(C) to develop__ the technique (using a recorder as backup if you wish) of selective note-taking. This involves writing down the key answers from an interview so that they can be transcribed easily afterwards. It is sensible to take down more than you think you'll need, but __(D) trying__ to get into the habit of editing out the material you are not going to need as the interview proceeds. It makes the material __(E) much__ easier and quicker to handle afterwards.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) seeing
(B) is
(C) to develop
(D) trying
(E) much

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
이 문장은 등위접속사 but을 기준으로 앞의 주절(It is sensible ~)과 뒤의 주절이 연결되는 구조입니다. 등위접속사 뒤의 절에서 'trying'을 쓰면 주어가 없는 상태에서 동사 역할을 하는 정동사 또한 존재하지 않게 되어 비문이 됩니다. 따라서 명령문의 형태를 취할 수 있도록 동사원형인 'try'로 고쳐야 올바른 문장이 됩니다.
