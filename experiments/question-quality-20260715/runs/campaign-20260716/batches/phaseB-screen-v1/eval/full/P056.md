## 원지문 (변형 전)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but spoils its local environment.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
A bridge is normally constructed __(A) to last__ one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark __(B) what shows__ the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it __(C) harmonizes__ with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must __(D) either__ adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but __(E) spoils__ its local environment.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) to last
(B) what shows
(C) harmonizes
(D) either
(E) spoils

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
(B) "what shows"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "that shows"로 고쳐야 한다. 정답인 (B) 문장의 골격은 주어 'Its presence', 동사 'makes', 목적어 'a lasting mark'로 이루어진 완결된 3형식 구조입니다. 목적어인 명사 'a lasting mark' 뒤에서 이를 수식하는 형용사절이 이어져야 하는데, 선행사를 자체적으로 포함하는 관계대명사인 'what'은 'a lasting mark'라는 선행사와 공존할 수 없어 문장 구조상 비문입니다. 따라서 선행사 'a lasting mark'를 수식하면서 주어 역할을 대신해 줄 수 있는 주격 관계대명사 'that' 또는 'which'로 고쳐야 합니다. 비록 뒤에 주어가 없는 불완전한 절('shows the evolution...')이 이어지더라도, 수식을 받는 명사 핵이 주절에 존재하므로 'what'은 사용될 수 없습니다.
