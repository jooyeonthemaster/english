# 문항 P037 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
KILLER

## 유형
GRAMMAR_ERROR

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented them with a list of words and asked them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. What he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can __(A) be influenced__ by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates __(B) that__ he was testing their language abilities. He presented them with a list of words and asked them __(C) to create__ a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. What he found was extraordinary. Those students __(D) who had been exposed__ to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders __(E) bent__ forwards, dragging their feet as they left, __(F) like if__ they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것을 고르시오.

## 선택지
(A) be influenced
(B) that
(C) to create
(D) who had been exposed
(E) bent
(F) like if

## 선언된 정답
(F)

## 해설
이 글의 마지막 문장에서 주절 뒤에 붙은 부사절은 주어가 실제로 그렇지 않음에도 마치 그런 것처럼 행동했다는 가정의 상황을 나타냅니다. 주어와 동사(they were)를 갖춘 완전한 절을 이끌며 부사절을 형성하는 접속사 자리에 전치사적 성격과 조건절 접속사가 혼종된 (F) 'like if'는 올 수 없으며, 가정법 접속사인 'as if'로 고쳐야 합니다. (A)는 명사구(human perception and behavior)를 주어로 삼아 수동 부사(influenced)의 수식을 받는 완결성 있는 수동태 구조가 올바르며, (B)는 동사 told의 간접목적어(a bunch of healthy undergraduates) 뒤에 명사절 접속사 that이 이끄는 직접목적어 절로 정문입니다. (C)는 asks A to-v 구조에 대응되는 5형식 목적격 보어 형태로 적절하며, (D)는 명사구(Those students)를 선행사로 삼는 주격 관계대명사 who가 이끄는 관계절 내에서 과거 완료 수동태(had been exposed)를 이루어 옳습니다. (E)는 명사(their shoulders)를 의미상 주어로 삼아 '자신의 어깨가 앞으로 굽혀진 채'라는 수동 관계를 나타내는 분사구조(bent)로 어법상 적절합니다.
