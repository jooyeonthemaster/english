## 원지문 (변형 전)
One of the most common mistakes made by organizations when they first consider experimenting with social media is that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program begins not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough understanding of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks because “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of itself. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either solve a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a measurable one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
One of the most common mistakes made by organizations __(A) when__ they first consider experimenting with social media __(B) is__ that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program begins not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough __(C) understood__ of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks because “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of __(D) itself__. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either __(E) solve__ a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a measurable one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) when
(B) is
(C) understood
(D) itself
(E) solve

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
문장의 'begins not with A but with B' 상관 구조에서 전치사 'with' 뒤에는 명사구가 이어져야 합니다. (C) 'understood'로 쓰면 수동의 과거분사가 되어 뒤에 이어지는 목적격 전치사구인 'of the organization’s own goals'의 수식을 문장 구조상 받을 수 없습니다. 따라서 전치사의 목적어 역할을 하면서 뒤에 명사구를 동반할 수 있는 명사 또는 동명사 형태인 'understanding'으로 고쳐야 전치사 with의 목적어 노릇을 명확히 수행할 수 있습니다.
