## 원지문 (변형 전)
Our emotions are thought to exist because they have contributed to our survival as a species. Fear has helped us avoid dangers, expressing anger helps us scare off threats, and expressing positive emotions helps us bond with others. From an evolutionary perspective, an emotion is a kind of "program" that, when triggered, directs many of our activities (including attention, perception, memory, movement, expressions, etc.). For example, fear makes us very attentive, narrows our perceptual focus to threatening stimuli, will cause us either to face a situation (fight) or avoid it (flight), and may cause us to remember an experience more acutely (so that we avoid the threat in the future). Regardless of the specific ways in which they activate our systems, the specific emotions we possess are thought to exist because they have helped us (as a species) survive challenges within our environment long ago. If they had not helped us adapt and survive, they would not have evolved with us.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Our emotions are thought to exist because they have contributed to our survival as a species. Fear __(A) has helped__ us avoid dangers, __(B) expressing__ anger helps us scare off threats, and expressing positive emotions helps us bond with others. From an evolutionary perspective, an emotion is a kind of "program" that, __(C) when triggered__, directs many of our activities (including attention, perception, memory, movement, expressions, etc.). For example, fear makes us very attentive, narrows our perceptual focus to threatening stimuli, will cause us either to face a situation (fight) or avoid it (flight), and may cause us to remember an experience more acutely (so that we avoid the threat in the future). Regardless of the specific ways __(D) in which__ they activate our systems, the specific emotions we possess are thought to exist because they have helped us (as a species) survive challenges within our environment long ago. If they __(E) did not help__ us adapt and survive, they would not have evolved with us.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) has helped
(B) expressing
(C) when triggered
(D) in which
(E) did not help

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
해당 문장은 과거 사실에 반대되는 상황을 가정하는 가정법 과거완료 구문입니다. 조건절의 통사 구조를 분석하면, 과거의 역사적 사실(long ago)에 대한 가정이므로 'If + 주어 + had + p.p.' 형식을 취해야 합니다. 따라서 단순 과거 시제인 did not help를 쓰면 주절의 'would not have evolved'와 시제 관계가 어긋나 비문이 됩니다. 이를 과거 사실을 가정하는 형태인 had not helped로 교정해야 어법상 올바른 문장이 됩니다. 주절의 조동사 과거형+have p.p. 형태를 보고 조건절의 시제를 역으로 추론하는 능력이 필요합니다.
