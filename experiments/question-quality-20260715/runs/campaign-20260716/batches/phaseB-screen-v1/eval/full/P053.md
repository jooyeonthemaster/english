## 원지문 (변형 전)
One of the most common mistakes made by organizations when they first consider experimenting with social media is that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program begins not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough understanding of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks because “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of itself. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either solve a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a measurable one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
One of the most common mistakes made by organizations __(A) when__ they first consider experimenting with social media __(B) is__ that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program begins not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough __(C) understood__ of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks because “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of __(D) itself__. In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) when
(B) is
(C) understood
(D) itself
(E) either

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
(C) "understood"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "understanding"로 고쳐야 한다. 이 문장의 구조는 전치사구인 'with a thorough understanding of...'에서 관사 'a'와 형용사 'thorough'의 수식을 받는 명사 핵이 필요한 자리입니다. 밑줄 친 'understood'는 과거분사 혹은 과거동사 형태로 명사구의 핵심 역할을 수행할 수 없으므로, 명사 역할을 하는 동명사 혹은 명사 형태인 'understanding'으로 고쳐야 합니다. 형용사의 수식을 직접 받으며 '조직의 목표에 관한 철저한 이해'라는 의미의 명사 핵으로 쓰인 'understanding'의 문법 성격을 파악하는 것이 중요합니다.
