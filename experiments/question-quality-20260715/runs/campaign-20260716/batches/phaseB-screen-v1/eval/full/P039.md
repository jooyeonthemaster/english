## 원지문 (변형 전)
Recording an interview is easier and more thorough, and can be less unnerving to an interviewee than seeing someone scribbling in a notebook. But using a recorder has some disadvantages and is not always the best solution. If the interview lasts a while, listening to it again to select the quotes you wish to use can be time-consuming, especially if you are working to a tight deadline. It is often more efficient to develop the technique (using a recorder as backup if you wish) of selective note-taking. This involves writing down the key answers from an interview so that they can be transcribed easily afterwards. It is sensible to take down more than you think you'll need, but try to get into the habit of editing out the material you are not going to need as the interview proceeds. It makes the material much easier and quicker to handle afterwards.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Recording an interview is easier and more thorough, and can be less unnerving to an interviewee than __(A) seeing__ someone scribbling in a notebook. But using a recorder __(B) have__ some disadvantages and is not always the best solution. If the interview lasts a while, listening to it again to select the quotes you __(C) wish__ to use can be time-consuming, especially if you are working to a tight deadline. It is often more efficient to develop the technique (using a recorder as backup if you wish) of selective note-taking. This involves writing down the key answers from an interview so that they can be transcribed __(D) easily__ afterwards. It is sensible to take down more than you think you'll need, but __(E) try to get__ into the habit of editing out the material you are not going to need as the interview proceeds. It makes the material much easier and quicker to handle afterwards.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) seeing
(B) have
(C) wish
(D) easily
(E) try to get

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
해당 문장은 동명사구인 'using a recorder'가 주어부의 핵심 핵(head)을 이루며 뒤에 이어지는 술어 동사부와 연결되는 구조입니다. 영어에서 동명사구 주어는 항상 단수 취급하므로, 복수형 동사인 'have'를 쓰면 수일치 규칙에 위배되는 비문이 됩니다. 따라서 복수형 동사 'have'를 단수형 동사 형태인 'has'로 교정해야 어법상 올바른 문장이 됩니다. 주어 내부의 명사구 'a recorder'가 단수 단어 형태를 띠고 있어서 혼동하기 쉬우나, 전체 주어의 본질은 기록기를 '사용하는 행위'라는 동명사구임을 파악하는 것이 핵심입니다.
