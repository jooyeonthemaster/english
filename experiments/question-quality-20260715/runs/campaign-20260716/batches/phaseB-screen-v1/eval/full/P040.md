## 원지문 (변형 전)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented them with a list of words and asked them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. What he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented __(A) them__ with a list of words and asked them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists __(B) was__ "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the __(C) wrinkled__ face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh __(D) was interested__ in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. __(E) That__ he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) them
(B) was
(C) wrinkled
(D) was interested
(E) That

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
해당 문장은 정동사 was 앞까지가 문장 전체의 주어 역할을 하는 명사절 자리를 구성하고 있습니다. 대명사 지시 관계에서부의 'found'는 목적어를 필요로 하는 타동사이나 뒤에 목적어가 빠진 불완전한 절이 이어지고 있으며, 선행사 또한 존재하지 않습니다. 따라서 완전한 절을 이끄는 대명사 지시 관계 접속사인 'That'은 이 자리에 올 수 없으며, 선행사를 자체적으로 포함하면서 불완전한 절을 이끄는 관계대명사 'What'으로 고쳐야 합니다. 문장 구조를 면밀히 분석하지 않고 단순 눈스캔으로 명사절의 시작을 That으로 판단하고자 하는 함정을 피해야 합니다.
