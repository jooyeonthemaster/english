## 원지문 (변형 전)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but spoils its local environment.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
A bridge is normally __(A) constructed__ to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark __(B) that__ shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit __(C) concerning__ __(D) how__ it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but __(E) spoils__ its local environment.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) constructed
(B) that
(C) concerning
(D) how
(E) spoils

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
문장의 본동사 자리 구별을 묻고 있습니다. 이 문장의 실질적인 주어는 'one of the first qualities(a bridge must exhibit는 관계사절)'에서 수식어구를 제외한 핵심 명사 'one'입니다. 따라서 구조적으로 이 문장에는 주어에 호응할 정동사(본동사)가 필요한 위치이므로 준동사인 concerning을 사용하면 본동사가 없는 비문이 됩니다. 따라서 단수 주어one에 맞추어 단수 동사형인 concerns로 고쳐 써야 합니다.
