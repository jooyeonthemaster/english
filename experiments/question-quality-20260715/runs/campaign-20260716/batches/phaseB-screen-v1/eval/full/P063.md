## 원지문 (변형 전)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented them with a list of words and asked them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. What he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented __(A) them__ with a list of words and asked them to create a coherent sentence from __(B) it__. One of the lists __(C) was__ "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh __(D) was interested__ in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. __(E) That__ he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) them
(B) it
(C) was
(D) was interested
(E) That

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
이 문장의 구조는 술어 동사 'was' 앞까지가 대명사 지시 관계 주어이며, 주어 역할을 하는 대명사 지시 관계에서부절(he found)은 타동사 found의 목적어가 비어 있는 불완전한 구조입니다. 접속사 'That'은 뒤에 필수 문장 성분을 모두 갖춘 완전한 절을 이끌어야 하므로 이 자리에 올 수 없습니다. 따라서 선행사를 내포하며 목적어 역할을 수행할 수 있는 관계대명사 'What'으로 고쳐야 합니다. 단순 접속사 대명사 구별과 달리 앞뒤 문장 구조를 한 통으로 분석해야 정답을 도출할 수 있습니다.
