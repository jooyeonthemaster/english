## 원지문 (변형 전)
If we understand critical thinking as: 'the identification and evaluation of evidence to guide decision-making', then ethical thinking is about identifying ethical issues and evaluating these issues from different perspectives to guide how to respond. This form of ethics is distinct from higher levels of conceptual ethics or theory. The nature of an ethical issue or problem from this perspective is that there is no clear right or wrong response. It is therefore essential that students learn to think through ethical issues rather than follow a prescribed set of ethical codes or rules. There is a need to encourage recognition that, although being ethical is defined as acting 'in accordance with the principles of conduct that are considered correct', these principles vary both between and within individuals. What a person values relates to their social, religious, or civic beliefs influenced by their formal and informal learning experiences. Individual perspectives may also be context dependent, meaning that under different circumstances, at a different time, when they are feeling a different way, the same individual may make different choices. Therefore, in order to analyse ethical issues and think ethically it is necessary to understand the personal factors that influence your own 'code of behaviour' and how these may coincide, alongside recognizing and accepting that the factors that drive other people's codes and decision making may be different.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) distinct
(B) is defined
(C) varies
(D) What
(E) different

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
해당 문장은 부사절 although 내에 삽입된 ‘being ethical is defined ~’ 구조 다음에 이어지는 주절의 구조를 지니고 있습니다. 여기서 주어는 복수 명사구인 ‘these principles’이며, 이에 술어 동사가 이어져야 하는 자리입니다. 복수 주어인 ‘these principles’에 호응하기 위해서는 동사 역시 복수형이어야 하므로, 단수형 동사 ‘varies’를 쓰는 것은 비문입니다. 따라서 단수 동사를 복수형인 ‘vary’로 교정해 써야 올바른 문장이 됩니다. 주어와 동사 사이에 여러 수식어가 길게 수반되지 않은 구조라 단순해 보이지만, 양태절과 부사절이 복잡하게 얽혀 있어 주어를 잘못 찾도록 유도한 함정이 있습니다.
