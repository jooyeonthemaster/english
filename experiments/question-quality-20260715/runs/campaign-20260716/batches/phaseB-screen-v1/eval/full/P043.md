## 유형

## 원지문 (변형 전)
One of the most common mistakes made by organizations when they first consider experimenting with social media is that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program begins not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough understanding of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks because “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of itself. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either solve a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a measurable one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
One of the most common mistakes __(A) made__ by organizations when they first consider experimenting with social media is that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program __(B) begins__ not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough understanding of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks __(C) because of__ “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” __(D) serves__ no purpose in and of itself. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either solve a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a measurable one). In all things, purpose __(E) drives__ success. The world of social media is no different.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) made
(B) begins
(C) because of
(D) serves
(E) drives

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
(C) "because of"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "because"로 고쳐야 한다. 해당 문장은 부사절 접속사가 이끄는 절이 주절의 근거를 나타내는 완결된 구조입니다. 밑줄 친 부분 뒤에는 주어인 'everyone else'와 동사구 'is doing'을 갖춘 완전한 절이 이어지고 있으므로 부사절을 이끄는 접속사 because가 올바릅니다. 전치사인 because of가 쓰이면 뒤에 정동사 절이 올 수 없어 비문이 되므로, because of를 접속사인 because로 고쳐야 합니다. 'of'의 유무에 따라 뒤에 명사구와 절이 오는 차이를 평소에 혼동하기 쉬우니 주의해야 합니다.
