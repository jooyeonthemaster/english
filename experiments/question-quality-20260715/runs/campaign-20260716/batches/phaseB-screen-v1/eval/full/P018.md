## 원지문 (변형 전)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but spoils its local environment.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark __(A) that__ shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must __(B) either adapting to or__ indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it __(C) makes__ a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in __(D) itself__ but __(E) spoils__ its local environment.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) that
(B) either adapting to or
(C) makes
(D) itself
(E) spoils

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
이 문장에서 조동사 must 뒤에 상관접속사구인 'either A or B'가 연결되어 동사원형의 병렬 구조를 이루어야 합니다. 문맥상 조동사 must에 걸려 병렬을 이루는 구조는 'adapt to'와 'indeed reinforce' 두 동사원형이어야 합니다. 따라서 준동사 형태인 'either adapting to or'는 이 자리에 올 수 없으며, 조동사 must의 원형 보어 역할을 할 수 있도록 'either adapt to or'로 고쳐야 합니다. 등위접속사 or 뒤에 동사원형인 reinforce가 버젓이 살아있는 것이 결정적인 정답의 단서입니다.
