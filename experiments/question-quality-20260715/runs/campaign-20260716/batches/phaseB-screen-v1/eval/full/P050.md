## 원지문 (변형 전)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but spoils its local environment.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must __(A) either adapt or__ indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it __(B) makes__ a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that __(C) works__ within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in __(D) itself__ but __(E) spoils__ its local environment.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) either adapt or
(B) makes
(C) works
(D) itself
(E) spoils

## 선언된 정답
(A)

## 해설
상관접속사 'either A or B'가 형용사구 및 동사구를 연결하는 병렬구조 문장입니다. 문맥상 'balance'에 '적응하거나(adapt to)' 그것을 '강화해야(reinforce)' 한다는 대조적 흐름을 이룹니다. 'reinforce'는 목적어를 직접 취하는 타동사인 반면, 'adapt'는 '~에 적응하다'라는 자동사로 쓰일 때 반드시 전치사 'to'를 동반해야 뒤에 오는 공통 목적어 'this balance'와 올바르게 연결됩니다. 따라서 'to'가 빠진 'either adapt or'는 비문이며, 'either adapt to or'로 고쳐야 합니다. 'adapt'가 타동사로 쓰여 뒤의 'balance'를 공통 목적어로 공유하는 구조로 착각하기 쉽지만, '균형을 적응시키다'라는 의미가 되어 흐름상 불가능합니다.
