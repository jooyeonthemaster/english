# 문항 P058 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
KILLER

## 유형

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented them with a list of words and asked them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. What he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment __(A) showing__ human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he __(B) was testing__ their language abilities. He presented them with a list of words and asked them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. What he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words __(C) took__ almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall __(D) than those__ who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders __(E) bending__ forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) showing
(B) was testing
(C) took
(D) than those
(E) bending

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
해당 문장은 부대형황을 나타내는 'with + 명사 + 분사' 구문을 취하고 있습니다. 명사구인 'their shoulders(그들의 어깨)'와 동사 'bend(굽히다)'의 관계를 분석해보면, 어깨는 스스로 무언가를 굽히는 동작의 주체가 아니라 학생들에 의해 굽혀지는 대상이므로 수동의 관계가 성립합니다. 따라서 능동을 나타내는 현재분사 bending은 어법상 비문이며, 수동의 의미를 완성하는 과거분사 bent로 고쳐야 올바른 문장이 됩니다. 분사구문의 의미상 주어와 분사의 태 관계를 평소에 능동 위주로만 파악하다가 신체 부위가 목적어로 올 때의 수동 관계를 간과하지 않도록 주의해야 합니다.
