## 지문
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that __(A) show__ the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit __(B) concerns__ how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. __(C) Depending on__ the type of bridge and the site, it is __(D) subordinate__ to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but __(E) spoils__ its local environment.

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) show

(C) Depending on
