## 지문
Recording an interview __(A) is__ easier and more thorough, and can be less unnerving to an interviewee than seeing someone scribbling in a notebook. But using a recorder has some disadvantages and is not always the best solution. If the interview lasts a __(B) while__, listening to it again to select the quotes you wish to use can be time-consuming, especially if you are working to a tight deadline. It is often more efficient __(C) to develop__ the technique (using a recorder as backup if you wish) of selective note-taking. This involves writing down the key answers from an interview so that they can __(D) be transcribing__ easily afterwards. It is sensible to take down more than you think you'll need, but try to get into the habit of editing out the material you are not going to need as the interview proceeds. It makes the material __(E) much__ easier and quicker to handle afterwards.
