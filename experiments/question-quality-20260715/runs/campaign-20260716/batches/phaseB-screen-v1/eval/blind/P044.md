## 지문
One of the most common mistakes __(A) made__ by organizations when they first consider experimenting with social media is that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program __(B) beginning__ not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough understanding of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks __(C) because__ “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” __(D) serves__ no purpose in and of itself. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either __(E) solve__ a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a measurable one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

(C) because
