## 원지문 (변형 전)
Learning a second language is easiest during early childhood. When children are exposed to two different languages from birth, they generally become native speakers of both. Adults, on the other hand, rarely attain the fluency of a native speaker in a second language. There is, however, some concern that bilingualism can cause problems for children. This is partly due to something called “code-switching,” which occurs when bilingual people mix two languages together. But code-switching can also be viewed as an extra ability that gives bilingual children cognitive advantages over their peers. Unlike other children, bilingual kids have another language from which they can retrieve a word if they can’t remember it in the first. Furthermore, shifting back and forth between languages can make it easier for them to switch between tasks, leading to superior problem-solving skills. So, although it may lead to moments of confusion, bilingualism could ultimately give children an advantage in terms of overall cognitive abilities.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Learning a second language is easiest during early childhood. When children are __(A) exposed__ to two different languages from birth, they generally become native speakers of both. Adults, on the other hand, rarely attain the fluency of a native speaker in a second language. There is, however, some concern that bilingualism can cause problems for children. ## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) exposed
(B) called
(C) from that
(D) to switch
(E) leading

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
정답 문장의 골격을 살펴보면, 선행사 'another language'를 수식하면서 동시에 전치사 'from'의 목적어 역할을 수행하는 관계사절이 이어지는 구조입니다. 관계대명사 'that'은 전치사 바로 뒤에 위치하여 쓰일 수 없으므로 밑줄 친 'from that'은 그 자리에서 명백한 비문입니다. 따라서 이를 전치사 뒤에 사용할 수 있는 목적격 관계대명사인 'from which'로 고쳐야 합니다. 사물 선행사만 보고 'that'이 맞다고 판단하기 쉬운 함정에 유의해야 합니다.
