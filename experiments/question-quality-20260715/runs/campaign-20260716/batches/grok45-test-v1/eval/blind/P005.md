## 지문
There will soon be many self-driving cars with internet access on the road. Although these cars have many benefits, experts warn that having more self-driving cars also means having more potential targets for hackers. Researchers recently proved through a demonstration that even today’s “smart” cars are __(A) vulnerable__. They used a computer __(B) to take__ control of a car while someone else was driving it. They were not only able to change the radio’s volume, __(C) adjust__ the air conditioner, and switch the windshield wipers on, but they were also able to take control of the transmission and stop the car as it was __(D) driving__ down the highway. Fortunately, it was just a demonstration, but it sends a clear message to both the auto industry and the government: automotive cybersecurity is a real issue that must be __(E) taking__ seriously.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) vulnerable
(B) to take
(C) adjust
(D) driving
(E) taking
