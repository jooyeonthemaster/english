## 지문
Gaining an audience for your writing involves not just finding a voice but ensuring that voice __(A) resonating__ with your intended readers. Entrepreneurs understand the importance of building a brand __(B) that__ makes them memorable and distinct from their competitors, __(C) drawing__ customers to their products or services. They achieve this by pinpointing their target audience, crafting a unique value proposition, and shaping a recognizable identity. While scholars might initially find these entrepreneurial concepts __(D) alien__, they actually engage in similar practices when they set themselves apart in their academic writing. Consider the typical literature review and motivation section of a scholarly article ― the aim is to highlight gaps in existing research and position oneself as the one __(E) who__ will address these overlooked areas. The challenge, as Labaree suggests, is not just in developing innovative ideas but in cultivating a distinctive voice that makes readers think, "Ah, this sounds like [the author]." It's about balancing the expression of your ideas with the development of a unique voice that leaves a temporary impression on your audience.
