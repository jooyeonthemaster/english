A clock can be almost any process that repeats __(B) itself__ over and over again for an indefinite period. Water clocks drip at a steady pace; quartz crystals vibrate regularly. But here too there is iteration ― the repeated burning of molecules of wax ― so this too is an iterative process, __(D) although__ at first masked. The use of radiocarbon dating is another, much longer scale clock that also appears to be like this. It seems to yield a smooth time scale but in fact does not: the decay of atoms of carbon-14 __(E) is__ repetitive, although on a large scale it gives the appearance of being continuous.
