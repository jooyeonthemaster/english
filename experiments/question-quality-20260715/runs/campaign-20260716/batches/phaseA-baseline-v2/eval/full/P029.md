## 원지문 (변형 전)
One of the most common mistakes made by organizations when they first consider experimenting with social media is that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program begins not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough understanding of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks because “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of itself. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either solve a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a measurable one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
One of the most common mistakes made by organizations __(A) when__ they first consider experimenting with social media __(B) is__ that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program begins not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough __(C) understood__ of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks because “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of __(D) itself__. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must __(E) either__ solve a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a measurable one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) when
(B) is
(C) understood
(D) itself
(E) either

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
(C) "understood"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "understanding"로 고쳐야 한다. 문맥상 전치사 but with 뒤에서 관사 'a'와 형용사 'thorough'의 수식을 받으면서 전치사구 'of the organization’s own goals...'의 수식을 동시에 받는 품사는 명사여야 합니다. 따라서 수집된 감정이나 인식 행위를 명사화한 'understanding'(이해)이 와야 하므로 수동을 의미하는 분사 형태인 'understood'는 이 자리에 올 수 없습니다. 형용사의 수식을 받으며 문장 구조상 직결되는 명사형태인 'understanding'으로 고쳐야 합니다. 동사 수식구조나 분사구로 착각하여 분사 수동태 형태인 'understood'를 정문으로 오해하지 않도록 주의해야 합니다.
