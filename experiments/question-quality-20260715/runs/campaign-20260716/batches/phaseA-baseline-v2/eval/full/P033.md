## 원지문 (변형 전)
People typically consider the virtual, or imaginative, nature of cyberspace to be its unique characteristic. Although cyberspace involves imaginary characters and events of a kind and magnitude not seen before, less developed virtual realities have always been integral parts of human life. All forms of art, including cave drawings made by our Stone Age ancestors, involve some kind of virtual reality. In this sense, cyberspace does not offer a totally new dimension to human life. What is new about cyberspace is its interactive nature and this interactivity has made it a psychological reality as well as a social reality. It is a space where real people have actual interactions with other real people, while being able to shape, or even create, their own and other people's personalities. The move from passive imaginary reality to the interactive virtual reality of cyberspace is much more radical than the move from photographs to movies.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
People typically consider the virtual, or imaginative, nature of cyberspace __(A) to be__ its unique characteristic. Although cyberspace involves imaginary characters and events of a kind and magnitude not __(B) seen__ before, less developed virtual realities have always been integral parts of human life. All forms of art, including cave drawings made by our Stone Age ancestors, __(C) involve__ some kind of virtual reality. In this sense, cyberspace does not offer a totally new dimension to human life. What is new about cyberspace is its interactive nature and this interactivity has made it a psychological reality as well as a social reality. It is a space __(D) which__ real people have actual interactions with other real people, while being able to shape, or even create, their own and other people's personalities. The move from passive imaginary reality to the interactive virtual reality of cyberspace is much more __(E) radical__ than the move from photographs to movies.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) to be
(B) seen
(C) involve
(D) which
(E) radical

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
밑줄 친 'which' 뒤의 문장 'real people have actual interactions with other real people'은 주절의 주어(real people), 동사(have), 목적어(actual interactions)를 모두 갖춘 완전한 형태입니다. 따라서 불완전한 문장과 결합하는 관계대명사 'which'는 이 자리에 쓸 수 없으며, 선행사 'a space'를 수식하면서 뒤에 완전한 절을 이끌 수 있는 관계부사 'where' 또는 전치사+관계대명사 형태인 'in which' 등으로 고쳐야 합니다.
