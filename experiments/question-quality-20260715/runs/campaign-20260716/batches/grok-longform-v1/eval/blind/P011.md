The potential contribution of greater economic success, if it involves vulnerable groups, cannot be denied. At the same time, it is important to _____, countries where a large part of the population derive their livelihood from uncertain sources cannot hope to prevent famines without specialized entitlement protection mechanisms involving direct public intervention. The real achievements of these countries lie in having provided direct public support to their populations in times of crisis.
