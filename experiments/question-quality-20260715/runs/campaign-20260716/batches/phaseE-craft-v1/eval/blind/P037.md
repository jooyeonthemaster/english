## 지문
Sweeping statements __(A) have__ been made about the effect of clothing on the behavior of a child. It has been said __(B) that__ a child who is better dressed is better behaved, and that a child who is dressed like a cowboy is louder than he is when dressed in ordinary clothes. Clothing, however, might have a temporary effect on the behavior of the child, but not a lasting effect. That is, we would predict that a child who was __(C) dressing__ in his best clothes and whose mother __(D) stressed__ that they were ‘best’ would be better behaved for a short period, but by the end of the hour the child would have forgotten his clothes and would be acting as he would in old clothes. Likewise, the child __(E) who__ is dressed in a cowboy outfit might be very noisy while playing cowboys for a brief time, but he might soon change to another activity and be no more noisy than usual.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) have
(B) that
(C) dressing
(D) stressed
(E) who
