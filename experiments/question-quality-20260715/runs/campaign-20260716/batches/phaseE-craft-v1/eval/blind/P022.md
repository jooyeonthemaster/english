Marc Isambard Brunel (1769—1849) is best known for the design and construction of the Thames Tunnel. Originally __(A) born__ in France, Brunel escaped to the United States during the French Revolution. He later moved to London. When the Napoleonic Wars were at their height, he invented machines for making boots. During the Napoleonic Wars, Brunel’s factory __(B) supplying__ British troops with boots. After the Wars ended, however, the government stopped __(C) buying__ his boots and he went out of business. A few years later, Brunel was imprisoned for several months __(D) because of__ his debt. At that time, London was very much divided by the River Thames and needed more ways for people and goods __(E) to move__ across it. In 1825, Brunel designed a tunnel under the river. The Thames Tunnel officially opened on 25 March 1843, and Brunel, despite being in ill health, attended the opening ceremony.
