## 원지문 (변형 전)
We know where we are headed and what we want to do. However, quite often, due to forces outside our control, things do not go as we had planned and we have to adjust to a postponement or create a whole new set of circumstances. This is what happens when life throws us a curveball. The fact is that life is unpredictable. For example, your car breaks down and you are late for an appointment. While it is true that you never arrive at that important meeting, you end up spending a few relaxing hours with people you would never have met otherwise. Remember that not only are curveballs the universe's way of keeping us awake ― which is a gift in and of itself ― but they are also its method of bringing us wonderful surprises.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
We know where we are headed and __(A) what__ we want to do. However, quite often, due to forces outside our control, things do not go as we had planned and we have __(B) to adjust__ to a postponement or create a whole new set of circumstances. This is what happens when life throws us a curveball. The fact is that life is __(C) unpredictable__. For example, your car breaks down and you are late for an appointment. While it is true that you never arrive at that important meeting, you end up __(D) to spend__ a few relaxing hours with people you would never have met otherwise. Remember that not only are curveballs the universe's way of keeping us awake ― __(E) which__ is a gift in and of itself ― but they are also its method of bringing us wonderful surprises.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) what
(B) to adjust
(C) unpredictable
(D) to spend
(E) which

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
‘end up’은 ‘결국 ~하게 되다’라는 의미를 나타낼 때 목적어 및 보어 역할을 하는 준동사로 동명사(-ing)를 취하는 성질이 있습니다. 따라서 (D)의 to부정사 ‘to spend’는 어법상 어긋나며, 이를 동명사인 ‘spending’으로 고쳐야 바릅니다. (D)는 ‘end up -ing’ 관용 표현 및 동사의 목적어 취사선택 메커니즘을 묻는 전형적인 문제입니다.
