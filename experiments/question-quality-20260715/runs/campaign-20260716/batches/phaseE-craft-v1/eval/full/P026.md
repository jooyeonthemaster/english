## 원지문 (변형 전)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they are controlled by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales is not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law that says each killing must take place as quickly and efficiently as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should be allowed to continue.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they are __(A) controlled__ by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales is not __(B) done__ for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber __(C) are distributed__ for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law that __(D) says__ each killing must take place as quickly and efficiently as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question __(E) whether__ this practice should be allowed to continue.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) controlled
(B) done
(C) are distributed
(D) says
(E) whether

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
주어인 'Most of the whale meat and blubber'에서, '부분을 나타내는 표현(Most of) + 명사' 구문은 뒤에 오는 명사의 수에 동사를 맞춥니다. 여기서는 불가산 명사인 'meat'과 'blubber'가 결합하여 추상적인 하나의 식량 자원 개념으로 단수 취급되었습니다. 따라서 복수 동사 'are distributed'는 어법상 틀렸으며, 단수 동사 'is distributed'로 고쳐야 합니다.
