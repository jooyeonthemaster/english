# 문항 P037 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
INTERMEDIATE

## 유형
GRAMMAR_ERROR

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Sweeping statements have been made about the effect of clothing on the behavior of a child. It has been said that a child who is better dressed is better behaved, and that a child who is dressed like a cowboy is louder than he is when dressed in ordinary clothes. Clothing, however, might have a temporary effect on the behavior of the child, but not a lasting effect. That is, we would predict that a child who was dressed in his best clothes and whose mother stressed that they were ‘best’ would be better behaved for a short period, but by the end of the hour the child would have forgotten his clothes and would be acting as he would in old clothes. Likewise, the child who is dressed in a cowboy outfit might be very noisy while playing cowboys for a brief time, but he might soon change to another activity and be no more noisy than usual.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Sweeping statements __(A) have__ been made about the effect of clothing on the behavior of a child. It has been said __(B) that__ a child who is better dressed is better behaved, and that a child who is dressed like a cowboy is louder than he is when dressed in ordinary clothes. Clothing, however, might have a temporary effect on the behavior of the child, but not a lasting effect. That is, we would predict that a child who was __(C) dressing__ in his best clothes and whose mother __(D) stressed__ that they were ‘best’ would be better behaved for a short period, but by the end of the hour the child would have forgotten his clothes and would be acting as he would in old clothes. Likewise, the child __(E) who__ is dressed in a cowboy outfit might be very noisy while playing cowboys for a brief time, but he might soon change to another activity and be no more noisy than usual.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) have
(B) that
(C) dressing
(D) stressed
(E) who

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
(C) "dressing"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "dressed"로 고쳐야 한다. 밑줄 친 (C)는 선행사 a child를 수식하는 관계절 내에서 과거시제 정동사 was에 이어지는 동사구의 일부입니다. 문맥상 아이가 옷을 '입혀진(입은)' 수동의 상태를 나타내므로 수동태를 구성하는 과거분사(dressed)가 와야 합니다. 만약 현재분사인 dressing을 쓰게 되면 '명사를 입히다'라는 능동의 의미가 되어 목적어가 필요해지나 뒤에 부사구만 이어지므로 어법상 틀립니다. 따라서 현재분사 dressing을 과거분사 dressed로 고쳐야 합니다. 'be dressed in'은 옷을 입고 있는 상태를 나타내는 관용적 수동태 표현임을 알아두면 좋습니다.
