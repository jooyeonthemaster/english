## 지문
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. __(A) Although__ they are controlled by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales __(B) is__ not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law __(C) that__ says each killing must take place as quickly and __(D) efficiently__ as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should __(E) allow__ to continue.
