## 원지문 (변형 전)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they are controlled by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales is not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law that says each killing must take place as quickly and efficiently as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should be allowed to continue.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. __(A) Although__ they are controlled by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales __(B) is__ not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law __(C) that__ says each killing must take place as quickly and __(D) efficiently__ as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should __(E) allow__ to continue.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) Although
(B) is
(C) that
(D) efficiently
(E) allow

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
정답 (E)가 속한 절은 whether this practice should ____ to continue 구조이다. 주어 this practice는 allow의 행위자가 아니라 허용의 대상이므로 수동 구조가 필요하다. 표시된 allow는 능동 원형이라 목적어도 없고 의미 관계도 성립하지 않아 이 자리에 올 수 없다. 따라서 be allowed로 고쳐야 한다. 조동사 뒤 원형만 보고 능동형을 고르면 함정이다.
