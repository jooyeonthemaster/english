## 원지문 (변형 전)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they are controlled by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales is not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law that says each killing must take place as quickly and efficiently as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should be allowed to continue.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although __(A) they__ are controlled by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales __(B) are__ not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law __(C) that__ says each killing must take place as quickly and __(D) efficiently__ as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should __(E) be allowed__ to continue.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) they
(B) are
(C) that
(D) efficiently
(E) be allowed

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
이 문장의 대절 내부 주어는 명사구 'the killing of whales'입니다. 전치사구인 'of whales'가 수식어로 뒤에서 주어의 핵을 수식하고 있지만, 실제 주어의 진짜 핵은 단수 명사인 'the killing'입니다. 따라서 복수 동사인 'are'는 이 자리에 올 수 없으며, 단수 동사 'is'로 고쳐야 합니다. 학생들은 바로 앞에 수식어로 붙은 복수 명사 'whales'를 주어로 착각하기 쉬우므로, 전치사를 사이에 둔 긴 주어의 진짜 핵을 찾는 연습이 필요합니다.
