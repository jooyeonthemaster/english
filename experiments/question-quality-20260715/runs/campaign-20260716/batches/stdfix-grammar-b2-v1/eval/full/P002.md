# 문항 P002 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
KILLER

## 유형

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Masks are too heavy to uphold indefinitely, and no matter how well you believe you are disguising yourself, others always know. The consequences are that you waste energy, sacrifice productivity, and lose people's trust. In our study, all of the CEOs were concerned that dropping their masks would make them appear weak. In every case, however, the leader's subordinates perceived the removal of the mask as an act of strength and courage. This, in turn, encouraged a culture where those subordinates did the same, creating a snowball effect. Some of the ways we helped the CEOs remove their masks were to actually name the masks, explore the significant personal and professional costs of upholding them, and ask the CEOs to reflect on the leader they most admired. At this point each of the CEOs was ready to rebuild a leadership identity based on values, strengths, and ambitions.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Masks are too heavy to uphold indefinitely, and no matter how well you believe you are disguising yourself, others always know. The consequences are that you waste energy, sacrifice productivity, and lose people's trust. In our study, all of the CEOs __(A) were concerned__ that dropping their masks would make them __(B) appear__ weak. In every case, however, the leader's subordinates perceived the removal of the mask as an act of strength and courage. This, in turn, encouraged a culture __(C) which__ those subordinates did the same, creating a snowball effect. Some of the ways we helped the CEOs remove their masks were to actually name the masks, explore the significant personal and professional costs of upholding them, and ask the CEOs __(D) to reflect__ on the leader they most admired. At this point each of the CEOs __(E) was__ ready to rebuild a leadership identity based on values, strengths, and ambitions.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) were concerned
(B) appear
(C) which
(D) to reflect
(E) was

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
정답이 속한 문장은 선행사 'a culture'를 수식하는 관계사절을 포함하고 있습니다. 관계사절 내부 구조를 분석하면 주어(those subordinates), 동사(did), 목적어(the same)를 모두 갖춘 완전한 문장입니다. 따라서 불완전한 절을 이끄는 관계대명사 'which'는 이 자리에 올 수 없으며, 앞의 선행사를 수식하면서 뒤에 완전한 절을 이끄는 관계부사 'where' 또는 '전치사+관계대명사' 형태인 'in which'로 고쳐야 합니다. 함정으로 관계사절 뒤의 분사구문 'creating...'을 절의 구성 요소로 착각하지 않도록 주의해야 합니다.
