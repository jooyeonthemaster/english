# 문항 P005 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
KILLER

## 유형

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Masks are too heavy to uphold indefinitely, and no matter how well you believe you are disguising yourself, others always know. The consequences are that you waste energy, sacrifice productivity, and lose people's trust. In our study, all of the CEOs were concerned that dropping their masks would make them appear weak. In every case, however, the leader's subordinates perceived the removal of the mask as an act of strength and courage. This, in turn, encouraged a culture where those subordinates did the same, creating a snowball effect. Some of the ways we helped the CEOs remove their masks were to actually name the masks, explore the significant personal and professional costs of upholding them, and ask the CEOs to reflect on the leader they most admired. At this point each of the CEOs was ready to rebuild a leadership identity based on values, strengths, and ambitions.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Masks are too heavy to uphold indefinitely, and no matter how __(A) well__ you believe you are disguising yourself, others always know. The consequences are that you waste energy, sacrifice productivity, and lose people's trust. In our study, all of the CEOs were concerned __(B) that__ dropping their masks would make them appear weak. In every case, however, the leader's subordinates perceived the removal of the mask as an act of strength and courage. This, in turn, encouraged a culture where those subordinates did the same, __(C) creating__ a snowball effect. Some of the ways we helped the CEOs remove their masks were to actually name the masks, __(D) exploring__ the significant personal and professional costs of upholding __(E) them__, and ask the CEOs to reflect on the leader they most admired. At this point each of the CEOs was ready to rebuild a leadership identity based on values, strengths, and ambitions.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) well
(B) that
(C) creating
(D) exploring
(E) them

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
(D) "exploring"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "explore"로 고쳐야 한다. 이 문장은 주어 보어로 기능하는 to 부정사구 내부에서 동사원형 세 개가 and로 나열된 병렬 구조(to actually name the masks, explore the costs, and ask the CEOs)를 이루고 있습니다. 따라서 두 번째 항목인 밑줄 부분은 동사 동명사/현재분사인 exploring이 될 수 없는 자리로 통사적 오류가 발생합니다. 그러므로 잘못된 exploring을 문맥상 등위 접속된 동사원형 explore로 수정해야 합니다. 표면에서 바로 앞의 전치사 of upholding과 헷갈려 동명사의 나열로 착각하기 쉬운 자리이므로 전체 구성의 병렬 핵을 찾아야 합니다.
