## 원지문 (변형 전)
Marc Isambard Brunel (1769—1849) is best known for the design and construction of the Thames Tunnel. Originally born in France, Brunel escaped to the United States during the French Revolution. He later moved to London. When the Napoleonic Wars were at their height, he invented machines for making boots. During the Napoleonic Wars, Brunel’s factory supplied British troops with boots. After the Wars ended, however, the government stopped buying his boots and he went out of business. A few years later, Brunel was imprisoned for several months because of his debt. At that time, London was very much divided by the River Thames and needed more ways for people and goods to move across it. In 1825, Brunel designed a tunnel under the river. The Thames Tunnel officially opened on 25 March 1843, and Brunel, despite being in ill health, attended the opening ceremony.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Marc Isambard Brunel (1769—1849) is best __(A) known__ for the design and construction of the Thames Tunnel. Originally born in France, Brunel escaped to the United States during the French Revolution. He later moved to London. __(B) When__ the Napoleonic Wars were at their height, he invented machines for making boots. During the Napoleonic Wars, Brunel’s factory supplied British troops with boots. After the Wars ended, however, the government stopped buying his boots and he went out of business. A few years later, Brunel was imprisoned for several months because of his debt. At that time, London was very much __(C) divided__ by the River Thames and needed more ways for people and goods __(D) to move__ across it. In 1825, Brunel designed a tunnel under the river. The Thames Tunnel officially opened on 25 March 1843, and Brunel, despite being in ill health, __(E) attending__ the opening ceremony.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) known
(B) When
(C) divided
(D) to move
(E) attending

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
등위접속사 and 뒤에 이어지는 절의 주어는 Brunel이고 목적어는 the opening ceremony입니다. 문장의 골격을 이룰 본동사가 반드시 필요한 자리이므로, 준동사인 attending을 쓰면 동사 역할을 할 수 없어 비문이 됩니다. 따라서 주어의 행위를 나타내는 정동사 과거형인 attended로 고쳐야 합니다. 'despite being in ill health'라는 삽입구 때문에 동사 자리를 놓치기 쉬우니 주의해야 합니다.
