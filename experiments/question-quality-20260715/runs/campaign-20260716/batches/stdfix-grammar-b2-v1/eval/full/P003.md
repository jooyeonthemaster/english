## 원지문 (변형 전)
Animals as well as humans engage in play activities. In animals, play has long been seen as a way of learning and practicing skills and behaviors that are necessary for future survival. In children, too, play has important functions during development. From its earliest beginnings in infancy, play is a way in which children learn about the world and their place in it. Children's play serves as a training ground for developing physical abilities - skills like walking, running, and jumping that are necessary for everyday living. Play also allows children to try out and learn social behaviors and to acquire values and personality traits that will be important in adulthood. For example, they learn how to compete and cooperate with others, how to lead and follow, how to make decisions, and so on.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Animals as well as humans engage in play activities. In animals, play has long __(A) been seen__ as a way of learning and practicing skills and behaviors that __(B) are__ necessary for future survival. In children, too, play has important functions during development. From its earliest beginnings in infancy, play is a way __(C) which__ children learn about the world and their place in it. Children's play serves as a training ground for developing physical abilities - skills like walking, running, and jumping that are necessary for everyday living. Play also allows children __(D) to try out__ and learn social behaviors and to acquire values and personality traits that will be __(E) important__ in adulthood. For example, they learn how to compete and cooperate with others, how to lead and follow, how to make decisions, and so on.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) been seen
(B) are
(C) which
(D) to try out
(E) important

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
주어 play를 수식하는 명사구 a way 뒤의 관계절 구조를 파악하는 문제입니다. 뒤따르는 절(children learn about the world and their place in it)은 주어인 children과 자동사 learn, 그리고 전치사구인 about the world...를 갖춘 완전한 절입니다. 따라서 불완전한 절을 이끄는 관계대명사 which는 올 수 없으며, 이를 전치사를 포함한 'in which' 형태로 고쳐야 올바른 문장이 됩니다. 선행사만을 보고 관계대명사를 성급하게 선택하도록 유도하는 함정이 있으므로 뒤따르는 절의 구조적 완전성을 꼼꼼하게 따져보아야 합니다.
