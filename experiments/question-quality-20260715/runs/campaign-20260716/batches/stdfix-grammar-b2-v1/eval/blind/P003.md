## 지문
Animals as well as humans engage in play activities. In animals, play has long __(A) been seen__ as a way of learning and practicing skills and behaviors that __(B) are__ necessary for future survival. In children, too, play has important functions during development. From its earliest beginnings in infancy, play is a way __(C) which__ children learn about the world and their place in it. Children's play serves as a training ground for developing physical abilities - skills like walking, running, and jumping that are necessary for everyday living. Play also allows children __(D) to try out__ and learn social behaviors and to acquire values and personality traits that will be __(E) important__ in adulthood. For example, they learn how to compete and cooperate with others, how to lead and follow, how to make decisions, and so on.
