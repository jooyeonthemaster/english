## 지문
Certain species are more crucial to the maintenance of their ecosystem than others. Such species, __(A) called__ keystone species, are vital in determining the nature and structure of the entire ecosystem. The fact __(B) that__ other species depend on or are greatly affected by the keystone species __(C) is revealed__ when the keystone species is removed. It is in this sense that we should draw attention to fig trees. Different species of fig trees may be keystone species in tropical rain forests. Although figs collectively produce a continuous crop of fruits, fruit-eating monkeys, birds, bats, and other vertebrates of the forest do not normally consume large quantities of figs in their diets. During the time of year when other fruits are less __(D) plentiful__, however, fig trees become important in sustaining fruit-eating vertebrates. Should the fig trees disappear, most of the fruit-eating vertebrates would be eliminated. __(E) Protects__ fig trees in such tropical rainforest ecosystems is an important conservation goal because it increases the likelihood that monkeys, birds, bats, and other vertebrates will survive.
