## 지문
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they are controlled by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands __(A) traditionally__ hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales __(B) is__ not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed for free to the local community, although some __(C) is sold__ in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law __(D) that__ says each killing must take place as quickly and efficiently as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should be allowed __(E) continuing__.
