In the film The Karate Kid, the teenaged Daniel asks the wise Mr. Miyagi to teach him karate. The old man agrees and orders Daniel first to wax his car in precisely opposed circular motions. Then he tells Daniel to paint his wooden fence in precise up and down motions. Finally, he makes Daniel hammer nails to repair a wall. Daniel is puzzled at first, then angry. He wants to learn the martial arts so he can defend himself. Instead he is limited to household chores. When Daniel is finished restoring Miyagi's car, fence, and walls, he explodes with rage at his "mentor." ## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
In the film The Karate Kid, the teenaged Daniel asks the wise Mr. Miyagi to teach him karate. The old man agrees and orders Daniel first __(A) to wax__ his car in precisely __(B) opposed__ circular motions. Then he tells Daniel to paint his wooden fence in precise up and down motions. Finally, he makes Daniel __(C) hammer__ nails to repair a wall. Daniel is puzzled at first, then angry. He wants to learn the martial arts so he can defend __(D) himself__. Instead he is limited to household chores. When Daniel is finished restoring Miyagi's car, fence, and walls, he explodes with rage at his "mentor." Miyagi physically attacks Daniel, who without thought or hesitation __(E) defending__ himself with the core thrusts and parries of karate. Through Miyagi's deceptively simple chores, Daniel has absorbed the basics of karate - without knowing it.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) to wax
(B) opposed
(C) hammer
(D) himself
(E) defending

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
선행사 Daniel을 부가적으로 설명하는 주격 관계대명사 who가 이끄는 관계사절 속에서, 삽입구인 전치사구(without thought or hesitation)를 제외하면 이 절 내에 술어 역할을 할 수 있는 진정한 동사(정동사)가 필요합니다. 하지만 밑줄 자리에 현재분사 형태인 defending을 사용하게 되면 관계사절 안에 본동사가 소실되게 되므로 비문이 됩니다. 따라서 선행사이자 관계사절의 주어 역할을 하는 3인칭 단수인 who에 맞추어 시제와 인칭을 반영한 정동사 형태인 defends로 고쳐 써야 합니다.
