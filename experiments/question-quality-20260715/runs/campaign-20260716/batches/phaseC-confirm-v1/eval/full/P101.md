## 원지문 (변형 전)
In most states, people who have been adopted are not allowed to have access to their files. They have no idea who their real parents are or why they were put up for adoption, and all their attempts to discover this information are met with firm resistance from the government. In short, adoption officials will tell them nothing. However, in the past decade, many adoptees have publicly protested this situation, and some states have changed their previous policies. Indeed, this change in adoption policy should take place nationwide. Adoptees need to know about their parents and their past, and those who do not care can simply refuse access to their files. Although restrictions against opening adoption files make the search much harder, they do not necessarily discourage those adoptees who want to discover who their biological parents are. Those men and women who are desperate to find what they want to know will, if they can afford it, hire a detective to find out. If not, some of them are willing to devote all their time and energy to finding more about their origins.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
In most states, people __(A) who__ have been adopted are not allowed to have access to their files. They have no idea who their real parents are or why they were put up for adoption, and all their attempts to discover this information __(B) are__ met with firm resistance from the government. In short, adoption officials will tell them __(C) nothing__. However, in the past decade, many adoptees have publicly protested this situation, and some states have changed their previous policies. Indeed, this change in adoption policy should take place nationwide. Adoptees need to know about their parents and their past, and those who do not care can simply refuse access to their files. Although restrictions against opening adoption files make the search much harder, they do not necessarily __(D) discourage__ those adoptees who want to discover who their biological parents are. Those men and women who are desperate to find what they want to know will, if they can afford it, hire a detective to find out. If not, some of them are willing to devote all their time and energy __(E) to find__ more about their origins.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) who
(B) are
(C) nothing
(D) discourage
(E) to find

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
밑줄 친 부분은 '자신의 시간과 에너지를 ~에 바치다'라는 뜻의 'devote A to B' 구문입니다. 여기서 'to'는 부정사를 이끄는 to가 아니라 전치사입니다. 따라서 전치사 뒤에는 명사나 동명사구 구조가 이어져야 하므로 'to find'는 어법상 틀리며, 'to finding'으로 고쳐야 올바른 표현이 됩니다. 학생들은 'be willing to' 구조에 익숙하여 'to find'를 동사원형으로 착각하기 쉽지만, 여기서는 devote A to B 구조임을 파악해야 합니다.
