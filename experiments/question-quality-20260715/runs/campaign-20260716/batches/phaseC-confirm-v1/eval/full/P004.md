# 문항 P004 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
INTERMEDIATE

## 유형
GRAMMAR_ERROR

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Sweeping statements have been made about the effect of clothing on the behavior of a child. It has been said that a child who is better dressed is better behaved, and that a child who is dressed like a cowboy is louder than he is when dressed in ordinary clothes. Clothing, however, might have a temporary effect on the behavior of the child, but not a lasting effect. That is, we would predict that a child who was dressed in his best clothes and whose mother stressed that they were ‘best’ would be better behaved for a short period, but by the end of the hour the child would have forgotten his clothes and would be acting as he would in old clothes. Likewise, the child who is dressed in a cowboy outfit might be very noisy while playing cowboys for a brief time, but he might soon change to another activity and be no more noisy than usual.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Sweeping statements have been __(A) made__ about the effect of clothing on the behavior of a child. It has been said that a child who is better dressed is better behaved, and that a child who is dressed like a cowboy is louder than he is when dressed in ordinary clothes. Clothing, however, might have a temporary effect on the behavior of the child, but not a lasting effect. That is, we would predict __(B) that__ a child who was dressed in his best clothes and __(C) whose__ mother stressed that they were ‘best’ would be better behaved for a short period, but by the end of the hour the child would have forgotten his clothes and would be acting as he would in old clothes. Likewise, the child who is dressed in a cowboy outfit might be very noisy while __(D) played__ cowboys for a brief time, but he might soon change to another activity and be no more __(E) noisy__ than usual.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) made
(B) that
(C) whose
(D) played
(E) noisy

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
① 문장의 골격 해부: 접속사 'while' 뒤에 이어지는 분사구문 자리입니다. 주절의 주어 'he'가 분사구문의 의미상 주어 역할을 합니다. ② 비문인 이유: 의미상 주어인 아이가 카우보이 놀이를 하는 능동의 의미이며 바로 뒤에 'cowboys'라는 목적어가 이어지므로 수동의 의미를 지니는 과거분사 played는 이 자리에 올 수 없습니다. ③ 따라서 played를 능동의 뜻을 갖는 현재분사 playing으로 고쳐야 합니다.
