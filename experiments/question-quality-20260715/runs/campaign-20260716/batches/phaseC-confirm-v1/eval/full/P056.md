# 문항 P056 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
INTERMEDIATE

## 유형
GRAMMAR_ERROR

## 원지문 (변형 전)
National tragedies create what psychologists call "flashbulb" memories, named for the vivid images that we retain: where we were when we got the news, how we learned it, how we felt, what we did. These memories are thought to be unforgettable, and it is true that the broad outlines of such catastrophes, thoroughly reported in the media, are well remembered, but your memory of your personal circumstances surrounding the events may not necessarily be precise. There have been numerous studies of this phenomenon, including surveys of fifteen hundred Americans' memories of the September 11 attacks. In this study, the respondents' memories were surveyed a week after the attacks, again a year later, and then again three years and ten years later. Respondents' most emotional memories of their personal details at the time they learned of the attacks are also those of which they are most confident and, paradoxically, the ones that have most changed over the years relative to other memories about 9/11.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
National tragedies create what psychologists call "flashbulb" memories, named for the vivid images that we retain: __(A) where__ we were when we got the news, how we learned it, how we felt, what we did. These memories are thought to be unforgettable, and it is true that the broad outlines of such catastrophes, thoroughly reported in the media, __(B) is__ well remembered, but your memory of your personal circumstances __(C) surrounding__ the events may not necessarily be precise. There have been numerous studies of this phenomenon, including surveys of fifteen hundred Americans' memories of the September 11 attacks. In this study, the respondents' memories were __(D) surveyed__ a week after the attacks, again a year later, and then again three years and ten years later. Respondents' most emotional memories of their personal details at the time they learned of the attacks are also those __(E) of which__ they are most confident and, paradoxically, the ones that have most changed over the years relative to other memories about 9/11.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) where
(B) is
(C) surrounding
(D) surveyed
(E) of which

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
문장의 주어와 동사의 수일치를 확인하는 문제입니다. 해당 절의 주어는 'the broad outlines of such catastrophes'이며, 중간에 삽입된 'thoroughly reported in the media'는 주어를 수식하는 분사구에 불과합니다. 주어의 핵이 복수 명사인 'outlines'이므로 동사 역시 복수형태가 되어야 합니다. 따라서 단수 동사 'is'를 복수 동사 'are'로 고쳐야 올바른 문장이 됩니다.
