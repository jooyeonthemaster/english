## 원지문 (변형 전)
National tragedies create what psychologists call "flashbulb" memories, named for the vivid images that we retain: where we were when we got the news, how we learned it, how we felt, what we did. These memories are thought to be unforgettable, and it is true that the broad outlines of such catastrophes, thoroughly reported in the media, are well remembered, but your memory of your personal circumstances surrounding the events may not necessarily be precise. There have been numerous studies of this phenomenon, including surveys of fifteen hundred Americans' memories of the September 11 attacks. In this study, the respondents' memories were surveyed a week after the attacks, again a year later, and then again three years and ten years later. Respondents' most emotional memories of their personal details at the time they learned of the attacks are also those of which they are most confident and, paradoxically, the ones that have most changed over the years relative to other memories about 9/11.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
National tragedies create __(A) what__ psychologists call "flashbulb" memories, __(B) named__ for the vivid images that we retain: where we were when we got the news, how we learned it, how we felt, what we did. These memories are thought to be unforgettable, and it is true __(C) that__ the broad outlines of such catastrophes, thoroughly reported in the media, are well remembered, but your memory of your personal circumstances surrounding the events may not necessarily be precise. There have been numerous studies of this phenomenon, including surveys of fifteen hundred Americans' memories of the September 11 attacks. In this study, the respondents' memories were surveyed a week after the attacks, again a year later, and then again three years and ten years later. Respondents' most emotional memories of their personal details at the time they learned of the attacks __(D) is__ also those __(E) of which__ they are most confident and, paradoxically, the ones that have most changed over the years relative to other memories about 9/11.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) what
(B) named
(C) that
(D) is
(E) of which

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
해당 문장의 주어부 핵은 전치사구의 수식을 받는 복수 명사 'memories'입니다. 수식어구에 포함된 단수 명사인 'the time'을 주어로 혼동하지 않는 것이 핵심입니다. 따라서 단수형 동사인 'is'는 복수 주어의 수일치 규칙에 어긋나므로 틀린 통사 표현입니다. 주어의 수에 맞춰 복수형 동사인 'are'로 고쳐 써야 정문이 됩니다.
