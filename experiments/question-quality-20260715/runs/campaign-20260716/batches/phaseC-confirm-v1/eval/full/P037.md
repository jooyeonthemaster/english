# 문항 P037 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
INTERMEDIATE

## 유형
GRAMMAR_ERROR

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Sweeping statements have been made about the effect of clothing on the behavior of a child. It has been said that a child who is better dressed is better behaved, and that a child who is dressed like a cowboy is louder than he is when dressed in ordinary clothes. Clothing, however, might have a temporary effect on the behavior of the child, but not a lasting effect. That is, we would predict that a child who was dressed in his best clothes and whose mother stressed that they were ‘best’ would be better behaved for a short period, but by the end of the hour the child would have forgotten his clothes and would be acting as he would in old clothes. Likewise, the child who is dressed in a cowboy outfit might be very noisy while playing cowboys for a brief time, but he might soon change to another activity and be no more noisy than usual.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Sweeping statements have been __(A) made__ about the effect of clothing on the behavior of a child. It has been said __(B) that__ a child who is better dressed is better behaved, and that a child who is dressed like a cowboy is louder than he is when dressed in ordinary clothes. Clothing, however, might have a temporary effect on the behavior of the child, but not a lasting effect. That is, we would predict that a child who was dressed in his best clothes and __(C) whose__ mother stressed that they were ‘best’ would be better behaved for a short period, but by the end of the hour the child would have __(D) forgetting__ his clothes and would be acting as he would in old clothes. Likewise, the child who is dressed in a cowboy outfit might be very noisy while __(E) playing__ cowboys for a brief time, but he might soon change to another activity and be no more noisy than usual.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) made
(B) that
(C) whose
(D) forgetting
(E) playing

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
조동사 뒤에 완료형인 'would have p.p.'가 와야 과거의 추측을 나타냅니다. 조동사 have 다음에 현재분사 'forgetting'이 올 수 없으므로 비문입니다. 따라서 'forgetting'을 과거분사인 'forgotten'으로 고쳐야 합니다.
