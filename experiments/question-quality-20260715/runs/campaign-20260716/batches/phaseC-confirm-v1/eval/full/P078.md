## 원지문 (변형 전)
Many countries have had legislation to protect animals in place for decades, even for centuries. This legislation may
regulate practices such as slaughter or experimentation, or may be more generally aimed at preventing cruelty. But
whether this kind of legislation is adequate lies at the heart of a key debate between those who argue, in a legal
context, for the promotion of animal welfare and those who argue for the promotion of animal rights. Those who argue
for animal welfare seek increased legal protection for animals from human-inflicted pain and other kinds of suffering
(for example, insufficient food and space). Animal rights advocates maintain that this welfare legislation does not go
far enough. Welfare legislation accepts, they argue, that animals may be used by humans for food and as experimental
subjects; it merely tries to curb the worst excesses of these practices. The problem, on the rights view, is more
fundamental than this: animals are not the kinds of thing that humans should use in this way at all. The problem,
therefore, is not one about treating animals inhumanely, but instead one about understanding them as human
resources.

## 발문
다음 글의 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은?

## 선택지
① failing to minimize the physical pain inflicted during scientific experiments
② granting animals the same moral and legal status that humans possess
③ viewing interactive living beings merely as instruments for human utility
④ relying on historic legislation that has lost its practical law-enforcing power
⑤ denying the biological distinctions between human and non-human species

## 선언된 정답
③

## 해설
이 글은 동물 복지론과 동물 권리론의 관점 차이를 비교하며 동물 권리론의 핵심 주장을 논하는 글입니다. 마지막 문장은 '동물을 비인도적으로 대하는 것이 문제가 아니라, 동물을 인간의 자원으로 이해하는 것이 문제'라는 권리론자의 핵심적 비판으로 결론을 맺고 있습니다. 핵심 문장인 'but instead one about...' 이하에는 동물을 인간을 위한 수단이나 자원으로 바라보는 도구적 관점을 지적하는 내용이 들어가야 합니다. 따라서 동물을 인간의 유용성을 위한 도구로만 취급하는 태도를 비판하는 ③이 빈칸에 가장 적절합니다. 글의 흐름을 정확히 읽었다면 매력적인 오답에 빠지지 않고 정답을 고를 수 있는 문항입니다.
