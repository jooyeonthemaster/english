# 문항 P019 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도

## 유형

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Sweeping statements have been made about the effect of clothing on the behavior of a child. It has been said that a child who is better dressed is better behaved, and that a child who is dressed like a cowboy is louder than he is when dressed in ordinary clothes. Clothing, however, might have a temporary effect on the behavior of the child, but not a lasting effect. That is, we would predict that a child who was dressed in his best clothes and whose mother stressed that they were ‘best’ would be better behaved for a short period, but by the end of the hour the child would have forgotten his clothes and would be acting as he would in old clothes. Likewise, the child who is dressed in a cowboy outfit might be very noisy while playing cowboys for a brief time, but he might soon change to another activity and be no more noisy than usual.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Sweeping statements have been made about the effect of clothing on the behavior of a child. It has been said that a child who is better dressed __(A) is better behaved__, and that a child who is dressed like a cowboy is louder than he is __(B) when dressed__ in ordinary clothes. Clothing, however, might have a temporary effect on the behavior of the child, but not a lasting effect. That is, we would predict that a child who was dressed in his best clothes and __(C) whose__ mother stressed that they were ‘best’ would be better behaved for a short period, but by the end of the hour the child would have forgotten his clothes and __(D) would have acted__ as he would in old clothes. Likewise, the child who is dressed in a cowboy outfit might be very noisy while __(E) playing__ cowboys for a brief time, but he might soon change to another activity and be no more noisy than usual.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) is better behaved
(B) when dressed
(C) whose
(D) would have acted
(E) playing

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
(D) "would have acted"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "would be acting"로 고쳐야 한다. 이 글의 밑줄 (D)는 지문에 명시된 'would have forgotten his clothes and would be acting' 부분에서 'would be acting'을 가리킵니다. 해당 문장은 가정의 조동사 would 뒤에 완료상(have forgotten)과 진행상(be acting)이 병렬 구조를 이루고 있으며, '옷을 잊어버린 상태이고, 행동하고 있을 것이다'라는 의미를 나타내는 어법상 올바른 구성입니다. 문제 오류 등으로 문맥상 오류가 없으므로 정답 및 밑줄 위치 조정이 필요할 수 있으나, 주어진 선지 중에서 (D)는 어법상 완벽한 문장 구조를 이룹니다.
