## 원지문 (변형 전)
Inefficient teachers overlook the potential power of the opening minutes of class. Often, if students are quiet enough and if there are many pressing demands on a teacher's time at that moment, more than ten minutes can disappear before class starts. It's no wonder that students are late for class; they have little reason to be on time. You can use the first ten minutes to get your class off to a great start, or you can choose to waste this time. The first minutes set the tone for the rest of the class. If you are prepared for class and have taught your students an opening routine, they can use this brief time to make mental and emotional transitions from the last class or subject and prepare to focus on learning new material. In summary, you should establish an opening routine to develop your class with an effective start.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Inefficient teachers overlook the potential power of the opening minutes of class. Often, if students __(A) are__ quiet enough and if there are many pressing demands on a teacher's time at that moment, more than ten minutes can disappear before class starts. It's no wonder __(B) that__ students are late for class; they have little reason __(C) to be__ on time. You can use the first ten minutes to get your class off to a great start, or you can choose to waste this time. The first minutes set the tone for the rest of the class. If you are prepared for class and have __(D) been taught__ your students an opening routine, they can use this brief time to make mental and emotional transitions from the last class or subject and __(E) prepare__ to focus on learning new material. In summary, you should establish an opening routine to develop your class with an effective start.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) are
(B) that
(C) to be
(D) been taught
(E) prepare

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
밑줄 친 (D)의 'been taught'는 어법상 틀린 표현입니다. 조건절의 주어 'you'가 학생들(your students)에게 도입부 루틴(an opening routine)을 가르치는 능동의 주체이고, 동사 뒤에 간접 목적어와 직접 목적어가 명확히 존재하므로 수동태를 쓸 수 없습니다. 따라서 'have'와 연결되어 능동의 현재완료 시제(have taught)를 이루도록 'taught'로 고쳐야 합니다. 앞의 'are prepared(수동태)'와 병렬 구조인 것처럼 착각하여 뒤에도 수동태인 been taught가 맞는 것으로 오인하기 쉬우나, 문맥과 목적어의 유무를 통해 능동태임을 판별할 수 있어야 합니다.
