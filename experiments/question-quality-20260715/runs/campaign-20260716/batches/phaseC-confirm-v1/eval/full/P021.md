## 원지문 (변형 전)
In the film The Karate Kid, the teenaged Daniel asks the wise Mr. Miyagi to teach him karate. The old man agrees and orders Daniel first to wax his car in precisely opposed circular motions. Then he tells Daniel to paint his wooden fence in precise up and down motions. Finally, he makes Daniel hammer nails to repair a wall. Daniel is puzzled at first, then angry. He wants to learn the martial arts so he can defend himself. Instead he is limited to household chores. When Daniel is finished restoring Miyagi's car, fence, and walls, he explodes with rage at his "mentor." Miyagi physically attacks Daniel, who without thought or hesitation defends himself with the core thrusts and parries of karate. Through Miyagi's deceptively simple chores, Daniel has absorbed the basics of karate - without knowing it.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
In the film The Karate Kid, the teenaged Daniel asks the wise Mr. Miyagi __(A) to teach__ him karate. The old man agrees and orders Daniel first to wax his car in precisely __(B) opposed__ circular motions. Then he tells Daniel to paint his wooden fence in precise up and down motions. Finally, he makes Daniel __(C) to hammer__ nails to repair a wall. Daniel is puzzled at first, then angry. He wants to learn the martial arts so he can defend himself. Instead he is __(D) limited__ to household chores. When Daniel is finished restoring Miyagi's car, fence, and walls, he explodes with rage at his "mentor." Miyagi physically attacks Daniel, __(E) who__ without thought or hesitation defends himself with the core thrusts and parries of karate. Through Miyagi's deceptively simple chores, Daniel has absorbed the basics of karate - without knowing it.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) to teach
(B) opposed
(C) to hammer
(D) limited
(E) who

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
문장의 뼈대를 살펴보면 사역동사인 makes 뒤에 목적어 Daniel이 오고, 그 뒤에 목적어가 직접 행동을 수행하는 능동 관계의 목적격 보어가 연결되어야 합니다. 사역동사는 목적어와 목적격 보어의 관계가 능동일 때 목적격 보어로 동사원형을 취합니다. 따라서 밑줄 친 (C)의 'to hammer'를 동사원형인 'hammer'로 고쳐야 올바른 문장이 됩니다.
