## 원지문 (변형 전)
We know where we are headed and what we want to do. However, quite often, due to forces outside our control, things do not go as we had planned and we have to adjust to a postponement or create a whole new set of circumstances. This is what happens when life throws us a curveball. The fact is that life is unpredictable. For example, your car breaks down and you are late for an appointment. While it is true that you never arrive at that important meeting, you end up spending a few relaxing hours with people you would never have met otherwise. Remember that not only are curveballs the universe's way of keeping us awake ― which is a gift in and of itself ― but they are also its method of bringing us wonderful surprises.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
We know where we are __(A) headed__ and what we want to do. However, quite often, due to forces outside our control, things do not go as we had planned and we have to adjust to a postponement or create a whole new set of circumstances. This is __(B) what__ happens when life throws us a curveball. The fact is that life is __(C) unpredictable__. For example, your car breaks down and you are late for an appointment. While it is true that you never arrive at that important meeting, you end up __(D) spending__ a few relaxing hours with people you would never have met otherwise. Remember that not only are curveballs the universe's way of __(E) kept__ us awake ― which is a gift in and of itself ― but they are also its method of bringing us wonderful surprises.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) headed
(B) what
(C) unpredictable
(D) spending
(E) kept

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
이 문항은 능수동의 구별 및 동명사의 목적어 취함 여부를 묻고 있습니다. 밑줄 친 (E)의 'kept'는 전치사 of 뒤에서 명사 역할을 하면서 뒤에 목적어 'us'를 취할 수 없으므로 비문입니다. 이는 우주가 우리를 깨어있게 유지하는 주체적이고 능동적인 의미이기 때문에, 뒤에 목적어(us)와 목적격 보어(awake)를 취할 수 있는 동명사형인 'keeping'으로 고쳐야 올바른 문장이 됩니다.
