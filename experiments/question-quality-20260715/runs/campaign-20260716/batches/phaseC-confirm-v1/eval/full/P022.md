## 유형

## 원지문 (변형 전)
You may be surprised to learn how long the shortest war in recorded history lasted. It was called the Anglo-Zanzibar War, and it lasted only 38 minutes. Why the war took place and the reason behind its brief duration are fairly interesting. The war broke out in 1896, between the United Kingdom and a small African island nation called Zanzibar. Before that time, the United Kingdom had a great deal of influence over Zanzibar, even choosing who the nation’s leader would be. However, when the leader of Zanzibar died in 1896, he was replaced by his cousin without the approval of the United Kingdom. The British ordered Zanzibar’s new leader to step down, but he refused. Rather than surrender, he gathered 3,000 soldiers and ordered them to protect his palace. Three British ships were sent in response. They soon began to attack the palace, quickly destroying the new leader’s army. The attack lasted for just 38 minutes, but it killed hundreds of Zanzibaris, forcing the new leader to flee and the country to immediately surrender.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
You may be surprised to learn __(A) how long__ the shortest war in recorded history lasted. It was called the Anglo-Zanzibar War, and it lasted only 38 minutes. Why the war took place and the reason behind its brief duration are fairly interesting. The war broke out in 1896, between the United Kingdom and a small African island nation called Zanzibar. Before that time, the United Kingdom had a great deal of influence over Zanzibar, even choosing __(B) who__ the nation’s leader would be. However, when the leader of Zanzibar died in 1896, he was replaced by his cousin without the approval of the United Kingdom. The British ordered Zanzibar’s new leader __(C) to step down__, but he refused. Rather than surrender, he gathered 3,000 soldiers and ordered them to protect his palace. Three British ships were sent in response. They soon began to attack the palace, quickly destroying the new leader’s army. The attack lasted for just 38 minutes, but it killed hundreds of Zanzibaris, __(D) forced__ the new leader to flee and the country __(E) to immediately surrender__.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) how long
(B) who
(C) to step down
(D) forced
(E) to immediately surrender

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
주절의 주어 'it'에 상응하는 정동사 'killed'가 이미 앞에 위치해 있고 문맥상 대등하게 병렬을 이루는 정동사를 추가하는 접속사가 없으므로, 쉼표 뒤에는 분사구문이 이어져야 합니다. 밑줄 친 forced는 과거분사 형태로서 뒤에 'the new leader'라는 명시적 목적어가 따르므로 능동의 관계를 나타내지 못해 비문이 됩니다. 따라서 이를 목적어를 취할 수 있는 능동 의미의 현재분사인 forcing으로 올바르게 고쳐야 합니다. (E)의 'to immediately surrender'는 'to flee'와 등위접속사 and로 연결되는 병렬 관계로서 완벽하게 복원되어 단일 정답이 굳어집니다.
