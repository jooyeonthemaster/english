## 원지문 (변형 전)
In most states, people who have been adopted are not allowed to have access to their files. They have no idea who their real parents are or why they were put up for adoption, and all their attempts to discover this information are met with firm resistance from the government. In short, adoption officials will tell them nothing. However, in the past decade, many adoptees have publicly protested this situation, and some states have changed their previous policies. Indeed, this change in adoption policy should take place nationwide. Adoptees need to know about their parents and their past, and those who do not care can simply refuse access to their files. Although restrictions against opening adoption files make the search much harder, they do not necessarily discourage those adoptees who want to discover who their biological parents are. Those men and women who are desperate to find what they want to know will, if they can afford it, hire a detective to find out. If not, some of them are willing to devote all their time and energy to finding more about their origins.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
In most states, people who have been adopted are not allowed to have access to their files. They have no idea who their real parents __(A) are__ or why they were put up for adoption, and all their attempts to discover this information are __(B) met__ with firm resistance from the government. Although restrictions against opening adoption files __(C) makes__ the search much harder, they do not necessarily discourage those adoptees who want to discover who their biological parents are. If not, some of them are willing to devote all their time and energy to __(E) finding__ more about their origins.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) are
(B) met
(C) makes
(D) what
(E) finding

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
문장의 주어는 명사구의 핵심어인 복수 명사 restrictions입니다. 'against opening adoption files'는 restrictions를 수반하는 수식어구에 불과합니다. 따라서 단수 동사인 makes를 복수 주어의 수에 맞추어 복수형 동사인`make`로 고쳐야 합니다. 전치사 against의 목적어로 쓰인 동명사구 'opening adoption files' 안의 동명사 opening이나 복수 목적어 files를 문장 자체의 주어로 오인하지 않도록 유의해야 합니다.
