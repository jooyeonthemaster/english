## 원지문 (변형 전)
Animals as well as humans engage in play activities. In animals, play has long been seen as a way of learning and practicing skills and behaviors that are necessary for future survival. In children, too, play has important functions during development. From its earliest beginnings in infancy, play is a way in which children learn about the world and their place in it. Children's play serves as a training ground for developing physical abilities - skills like walking, running, and jumping that are necessary for everyday living. Play also allows children to try out and learn social behaviors and to acquire values and personality traits that will be important in adulthood. For example, they learn how to compete and cooperate with others, how to lead and follow, how to make decisions, and so on.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Animals as well as humans __(A) engage__ in play activities. In animals, play has long been __(B) seen__ as a way of learning and practicing skills and behaviors that are necessary for future survival. In children, too, play has important functions during development. From its earliest beginnings in infancy, play is a way __(C) which__ children learn about the world and their place in it. Children's play serves as a training ground for developing physical abilities - skills like walking, running, and __(D) jumping__ that are necessary for everyday living. Play also allows children __(E) to try__ out and learn social behaviors and to acquire values and personality traits that will be important in adulthood. For example, they learn how to compete and cooperate with others, how to lead and follow, how to make decisions, and so on.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) engage
(B) seen
(C) which
(D) jumping
(E) to try

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
(C)가 포함된 문장의 구조를 살펴보면, 'play is a way' 뒤에 수식절이 이어지는 구조입니다. 관계대명사 which 뒤에는 주어나 목적어가 빠진 불완전한 문장이 와야 하지만, ‘children(주어) learn(동사) about the world and their place in it(전치사구)’ 구조의 완전한 절이 이어지고 있습니다. 따라서 관계대명사 단독 형태인 which를 쓸 수 없으며, 선행사 a way에 호응하여 뒤의 완전한 절을 이끌 수 있는 'in which'로 고쳐 써야 안전하고 유효한 어법이 됩니다.
