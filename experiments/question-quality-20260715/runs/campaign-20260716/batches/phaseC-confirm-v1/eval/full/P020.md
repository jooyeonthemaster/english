## 원지문 (변형 전)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they are controlled by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales is not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law that says each killing must take place as quickly and efficiently as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should be allowed to continue.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they __(A) are controlled__ by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales is not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber __(B) is distributed__ for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are __(C) held__ each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law that says each killing must take place as quickly and __(D) efficiently__ as possible. The government claims __(E) what__ the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should be allowed to continue.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) are controlled
(B) is distributed
(C) held
(D) efficiently
(E) what

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
동사 claims의 목적어로 명사절 두 개가 병렬 접속사 and에 의해 대등하게 연결되어 있습니다. 두 번째 목적어절이 'and that this is...'로 완전한 명사절 접속사 that을 취하고 있는 구조에서, 첫 번째 목적어절을 이끄는 (E) 역시 완전한 구조인 'the average annual catch(주어) is(동사) only about 800 whales(보어)'를 이끌어 넘겨야 하므로 선행사를 포함한 관계대명사인 what은 올 수 없습니다. 따라서 선행사 없이 완전한 절을 이끄는 명사절 접속사인 that으로 고쳐야 합니다.
