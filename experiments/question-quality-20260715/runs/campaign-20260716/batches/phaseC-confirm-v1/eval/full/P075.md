# 문항 P075 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도

## 유형

## 원지문 (변형 전)
You may be surprised to learn how long the shortest war in recorded history lasted. It was called the Anglo-Zanzibar War, and it lasted only 38 minutes. Why the war took place and the reason behind its brief duration are fairly interesting. The war broke out in 1896, between the United Kingdom and a small African island nation called Zanzibar. Before that time, the United Kingdom had a great deal of influence over Zanzibar, even choosing who the nation’s leader would be. However, when the leader of Zanzibar died in 1896, he was replaced by his cousin without the approval of the United Kingdom. The British ordered Zanzibar’s new leader to step down, but he refused. Rather than surrender, he gathered 3,000 soldiers and ordered them to protect his palace. Three British ships were sent in response. They soon began to attack the palace, quickly destroying the new leader’s army. The attack lasted for just 38 minutes, but it killed hundreds of Zanzibaris, forcing the new leader to flee and the country to immediately surrender.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
You may be surprised to learn how long the shortest war in recorded history __(A) lasted__. It was called the Anglo-Zanzibar War, and it lasted only 38 minutes. Why the war took place and the reason behind its brief duration __(B) are__ fairly interesting. The war broke out in 1896, between the United Kingdom and a small African island nation __(C) called__ Zanzibar. Before that time, the United Kingdom had a great deal of influence over Zanzibar, even choosing who the nation’s leader would be. However, when the leader of Zanzibar died in 1896, he was replaced by his cousin without the approval of the United Kingdom. The British ordered Zanzibar’s new leader __(D) to step__ down, but he refused. Rather than surrender, he gathered 3,000 soldiers and ordered them to protect his palace. Three British ships were sent in response. They soon began to attack the palace, quickly destroying the new leader’s army. The attack lasted for just 38 minutes, but it killed hundreds of Zanzibaris, __(E) forced__ the new leader to flee and the country to immediately surrender.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) lasted
(B) are
(C) called
(D) to step
(E) forced

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
이 문장은 등위접속사 'but'으로 시작되는 절('it killed hundreds of Zanzibaris') 뒤에 콤마가 있고, 별도의 연결어 없이 연속적인 사건이나 결과를 나타내야 하는 위치입니다. 만약 이 자리에 정동사의 과거형인 'forced'가 오게 되면 등위접속사 없이 동사 'killed'와 중첩되므로 통사적 오류가 발생합니다. 또한 뒤에 목적어인 'the new leader'가 존재하므로, 수동의 의미를 나타내는 과거분사로 볼 수도 없습니다. 따라서 주절의 보충 성분 역할을 하면서 목적어를 취할 수 있는 능동형의 현재분사구문인 'forcing'으로 수정해야 합니다.
