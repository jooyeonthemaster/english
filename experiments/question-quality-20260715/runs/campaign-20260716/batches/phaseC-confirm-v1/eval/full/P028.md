## 원지문 (변형 전)
Every year on the second Monday of October, the United States celebrates Columbus Day. This holiday honors Christopher Columbus, the Italian explorer who is credited with discovering America on October 12, 1492. However, considering his mistreatment of Native Americans and the devastating effect of his arrival, honoring the explorer does not make any sense at all. After arriving on the continent, Columbus killed and enslaved thousands of Native Americans. Additionally, the diseases that Columbus and other Europeans brought with them severely reduced the population of Native Americans. Therefore, the entire country should support the creation of a new holiday that honors Native Americans and remembers their suffering. As an alternative to Columbus Day, Americans should celebrate Native American Day or Indigenous Peoples’ Day.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Every year on the second Monday of October, the United States celebrates Columbus Day. This holiday honors Christopher Columbus, the Italian explorer who __(A) is credited__ with discovering America on October 12, 1492. However, __(B) considering__ his mistreatment of Native Americans and the devastating effect of his arrival, __(C) honoring__ the explorer does not make any sense at all. After arriving on the continent, Columbus killed and enslaved thousands of Native Americans. Additionally, the diseases that Columbus and other Europeans brought with them severely __(D) reducing__ the population of Native Americans. Therefore, the entire country should support the creation of a new holiday that honors Native Americans and __(E) remembers__ their suffering. As an alternative to Columbus Day, Americans should celebrate Native American Day or Indigenous Peoples’ Day.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) is credited
(B) considering
(C) honoring
(D) reducing
(E) remembers

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
이 문장의 진짜 주어는 관계대명사절 'that Columbus and other Europeans brought with them'의 수식을 받는 복수 명사 'the diseases'입니다. 따라서 이 주어에 호응하여 문장의 서술어 역할을 하는 과거시제 정동사(본동사)가 필요한 자리입니다. 하지만 문맥 속에 'reducing'과 같은 준동사가 쓰이면 해당 문장에 정동사가 아예 존재하지 않는 비문이 됩니다. 따라서 과거형 정동사인 'reduced'로 고쳐야 올바른 문장이 됩니다.
