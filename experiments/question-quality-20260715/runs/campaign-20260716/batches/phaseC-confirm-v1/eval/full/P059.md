## 원지문 (변형 전)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they are controlled by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales is not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law that says each killing must take place as quickly and efficiently as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should be allowed to continue.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they are __(A) controlled__ by Denmark, they have __(B) their__ own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales is not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber __(C) distributing__ for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law that says each killing must take place as __(D) quickly__ and efficiently as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question __(E) whether__ this practice should be allowed to continue.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) controlled
(B) their
(C) distributing
(D) quickly
(E) whether

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
(C) "distributing"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "is distributed"로 고쳐야 한다. 정답인 (C)가 포함된 문장은 'Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed...'로, 원래 지문에서는 올바른 정동사 'is distributed'가 사용되었습니다. 하지만 이 문제는 (C) 자리에 준동사인 'distributing'을 제시하여 정동사 자리에 잘못 쓰인 것을 찾아내도록 설계되었습니다. 'Most of + 단수명사구(the whale meat and blubber)'는 단수 취급하므로, 문장의 본동사 역할을 하면서 수동의 의미를 나타내는 single 정동사인 'is distributed'로 고쳐야 어법상 올바릅니다.
