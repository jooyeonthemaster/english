## 원지문 (변형 전)
Joseph B. Friedman was an independent American inventor with a broad range of interests and ideas. By the age of fourteen, he had conceptualized his first invention, the lighted pencil, and was attempting to market his idea. Over the course of his inventing career, he would experiment with various ideas. He was issued nine U.S. patents and held patents in Great Britain, Australia and Canada. His first patent was issued for improvements to the fountain pen on April 18, 1922. This was also the first invention that he successfully sold to Sheaffer Pen Company in the mid 1930s. In the 1920s, Friedman began his education in real estate. Although he was working as a real estate agent in San Francisco, California, the 1930s proved to be his most productive patenting period, with six of his nine U.S. patents being issued then. One of these patents was his most successful invention - the flexible drinking straw.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Joseph B. Friedman was an independent American inventor with a broad range of interests and ideas. By the age of fourteen, he had __(A) conceptualized__ his first invention, the lighted pencil, and was attempting to market his idea. Over the course of his inventing career, he would experiment with various ideas. He was issued nine U.S. patents and held patents in Great Britain, Australia and Canada. His first patent was issued for improvements to the fountain pen on April 18, 1922. This was also the first invention __(B) that__ he successfully sold to Sheaffer Pen Company in the mid 1930s. In the 1920s, Friedman began his education in real estate. __(C) Although__ he was working as a real estate agent in San Francisco, California, the 1930s __(D) proving__ to be his most productive patenting period, with six of his nine U.S. patents __(E) being issued__ then. One of these patents was his most successful invention - the flexible drinking straw.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) conceptualized
(B) that
(C) Although
(D) proving
(E) being issued

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
정답 문장은 접속사 Although가 이끄는 부사절이 끝난 후 이어지는 주절입니다.
주절의 진짜 주어는 'the 1930s'이며, 밑줄 친 부분은 주절의 정동사가 자리할 곳이므로 현재분사인 proving은 비문입니다.
따라서 접속사절의 시제(과거)와 문맥에 맞게 proving을 과거 정동사인 proved로 고쳐야 합니다.
