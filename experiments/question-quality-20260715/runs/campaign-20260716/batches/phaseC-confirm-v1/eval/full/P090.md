## 원지문 (변형 전)
Masks are too heavy to uphold indefinitely, and no matter how well you believe you are disguising yourself, others always know. The consequences are that you waste energy, sacrifice productivity, and lose people's trust. In our study, all of the CEOs were concerned that dropping their masks would make them appear weak. In every case, however, the leader's subordinates perceived the removal of the mask as an act of strength and courage. This, in turn, encouraged a culture where those subordinates did the same, creating a snowball effect. Some of the ways we helped the CEOs remove their masks were to actually name the masks, explore the significant personal and professional costs of upholding them, and ask the CEOs to reflect on the leader they most admired. At this point each of the CEOs was ready to rebuild a leadership identity based on values, strengths, and ambitions.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Masks are too heavy to uphold indefinitely, and no matter how well you believe you are disguising yourself, others always know. The consequences are that you waste energy, sacrifice productivity, and lose people's trust. In our study, all of the CEOs were concerned that __(A) dropping__ their masks would make them appear weak. In every case, however, the leader's subordinates __(B) perceived__ the removal of the mask as an act of strength and courage. This, in turn, encouraged a culture where those subordinates did the same, __(C) creating__ a snowball effect. Some of the ways we helped the CEOs remove their masks __(D) being__ to actually name the masks, explore the significant personal and professional costs of upholding them, and ask the CEOs to reflect on the leader they most admired. At this point each of the CEOs was ready to rebuild a leadership identity __(E) based__ on values, strengths, and ambitions.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) dropping
(B) perceived
(C) creating
(D) being
(E) based

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
주어로 쓰인 'Some of the ways'(관계사절 'we helped the CEOs remove their masks'의 수식을 받음)에서 핵심 주어는 복수 대명사 'Some'입니다. 따라서 문장 전체의 서술어 역할을 할 복수형 정동사가 필요한 자리입니다. 그러나 (D)의 'being'은 준동사(현재분사/동명사)이므로 문장을 완결할 수 없게 만들어 비문이 됩니다. 따라서 복수 주어 Some에 수일치되면서 과거 시제를 나타낼 수 있는 복수형 정동사 'were'로 고쳐야 합니다.
