## 지문
We know where we are headed and __(A) what__ we want to do. However, quite often, due to forces outside our control, things do not go as we __(B) had planned__ and we have to adjust to a postponement or create a whole new set of circumstances. This is what happens when life throws us a curveball. The fact is that life is __(C) unpredictable__. For example, your car breaks down and you are late for an appointment. While it is true that you never arrive at that important meeting, you end up __(D) spending__ a few relaxing hours with people you would never have met otherwise. Remember that not only are curveballs the universe's way of keeping us __(E) to awake__ ― which is a gift in and of itself ― but they are also its method of bringing us wonderful surprises.
