# 문항 P069

## 지문
Most people attack a new problem by relying heavily on the tools and skills that are most familiar to them. While this approach can work well for problems that are similar to those previously solved, it often fails, and fails miserably, when a new problem is particularly novel. In this circumstance, it is best to assume nothing and treat the problem as if you have never seen anything like it before. In martial arts, this sense of looking freshly at something is known as 'beginner's mind.' Beginners to any art don't know what is important and what is irrelevant, so they try to absorb every detail. Experienced martial artists use their experience as a filter to separate the essential from the irrelevant. When that filter _____, then even seasoned masters can make mistakes.

## 발문
다음 글의 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은?

## 선택지
1 blindly discards crucial elements based on past habits
2 fails to apply the beginner's mind consistently
3 treats familiar tools as irrelevant compared to new ones
4 encourages them to absorb every given detail
5 blocks out unfamiliar situations altogether
