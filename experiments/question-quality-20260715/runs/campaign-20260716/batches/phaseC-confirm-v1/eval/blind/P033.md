## 지문
Inefficient teachers overlook the potential power of the opening minutes of class. Often, if students __(A) are__ quiet enough and if there are many pressing demands on a teacher's time at that moment, more than ten minutes can disappear before class starts. It's no wonder __(B) that__ students are late for class; they have little reason __(C) to be__ on time. You can use the first ten minutes to get your class off to a great start, or you can choose to waste this time. The first minutes set the tone for the rest of the class. If you are prepared for class and have __(D) been taught__ your students an opening routine, they can use this brief time to make mental and emotional transitions from the last class or subject and __(E) prepare__ to focus on learning new material. In summary, you should establish an opening routine to develop your class with an effective start.
