# 문항 P066

## 지문
We must be careful when looking at proverbs as expressing aspects of a certain worldview or mentality of a people. That is, _____. There are so many popular proverbs from classical, Biblical, and medieval times current in various cultures that it would be foolish to think of them as showing some imagined national character. Nevertheless, the frequent use of certain proverbs in a particular culture could be used together with other social and cultural indicators to form some common concepts. Thus, if the Germans really do use the proverb, "Morgenstunde hat Gold im Munde" (The morning hour has gold in its mouth) with high frequency, then it does mirror at least to some degree the German attitude towards getting up early.
