## 지문
Some actors completely immerse themselves in their roles by using a technique called method acting. Method actors seem to actually become the characters they play, and they may even continue playing the role after the cameras have been turned off. If necessary, they will change their sleeping patterns or their appearance, with the ultimate goal of _____. Each role becomes a journey for the actor to explore a whole new identity. It can cause serious psychological and behavioral changes, sometimes even resulting in emotional trauma. In order to give the best performance possible, method actors step out of their own comfort zone and into the character’s shoes. As a result, audiences feel like they are watching real people doing real things, not just fictional characters in a movie.
