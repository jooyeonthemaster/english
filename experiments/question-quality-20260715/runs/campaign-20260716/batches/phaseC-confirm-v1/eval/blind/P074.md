# 문항 P074

## 지문
In the film The Karate Kid, the teenaged Daniel asks the wise Mr. Miyagi to teach him karate. The old man agrees and orders Daniel first to wax his car in __(A) precisely__ opposed circular motions. Then he tells Daniel __(B) paint__ his wooden fence in precise up and down motions. Finally, he makes Daniel __(C) hammer__ nails to repair a wall. Daniel is puzzled at first, then angry. He wants to learn the martial arts so he can defend himself. Instead he is limited to household chores. When Daniel is finished __(D) restoring__ Miyagi's car, fence, and walls, he explodes with rage at his "mentor." Miyagi physically attacks Daniel, __(E) who__ without thought or hesitation defends himself with the core thrusts and parries of karate. Through Miyagi's deceptively simple chores, Daniel has absorbed the basics of karate - without knowing it.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) precisely
(B) paint
(C) hammer
(D) restoring
(E) who
