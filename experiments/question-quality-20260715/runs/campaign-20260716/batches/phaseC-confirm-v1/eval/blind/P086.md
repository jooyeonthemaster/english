# 문항 P086

## 지문
Other people's views and troubles can spread like a virus. Don't damage yourself by adopting negative, unproductive attitudes when you talk with others. If you encounter a sorrowful friend, or a colleague who has suffered sudden trouble, be careful not to be overcome by the misfortune. Remember to discriminate between events themselves and your interpretations of them. Remind yourself: "What hurts this person is not the occurrence itself but the response he or she has uncritically adopted." We do a good service to others by remaining detached and avoiding melodramatic reactions. Still, if you find yourself in a conversation with someone who is depressed, hurt, or frustrated, show them kindness and give them a sympathetic ear; just don't _____.
