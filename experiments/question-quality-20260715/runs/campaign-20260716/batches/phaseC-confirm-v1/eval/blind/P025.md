## 지문
In the film The Karate Kid, the teenaged Daniel asks the wise Mr. Miyagi to teach him karate. The old man agrees and orders Daniel first __(A) to wax__ his car in precisely __(B) opposed__ circular motions. Then he tells Daniel to paint his wooden fence in precise up and down motions. Finally, he makes Daniel __(C) hammer__ nails to repair a wall. Daniel is puzzled at first, then angry. He wants to learn the martial arts so he can defend __(D) himself__. Instead he is limited to household chores. When Daniel is finished restoring Miyagi's car, fence, and walls, he explodes with rage at his "mentor." Miyagi physically attacks Daniel, who without thought or hesitation __(E) defending__ himself with the core thrusts and parries of karate. Through Miyagi's deceptively simple chores, Daniel has absorbed the basics of karate - without knowing it.
