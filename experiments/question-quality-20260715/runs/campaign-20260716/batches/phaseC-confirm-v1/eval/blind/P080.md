## 지문
A way to get things done more efficiently and get better results is to do the right thing at the right time of day. Know your own body rhythm, respect your internal clock, and pay attention to how your energy level ebbs and flows during the day. If you have lots of energy early in the morning, that is when you should schedule difficult activities, whether for you these are brainstorming, writing, or practicing. Use the same principle in planning activities for your team. Schedule intervals of productive time and breaks so that you get the most from people.
