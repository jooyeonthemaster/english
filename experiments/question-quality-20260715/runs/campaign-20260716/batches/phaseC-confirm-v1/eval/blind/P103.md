## 지문
Animals as well as humans __(A) engage__ in play activities. In animals, play has long been __(B) seen__ as a way of learning and practicing skills and behaviors that are necessary for future survival. In children, too, play has important functions during development. From its earliest beginnings in infancy, play is a way __(C) which__ children learn about the world and their place in it. Children's play serves as a training ground for developing physical abilities - skills like walking, running, and __(D) jumping__ that are necessary for everyday living. Play also allows children __(E) to try__ out and learn social behaviors and to acquire values and personality traits that will be important in adulthood. For example, they learn how to compete and cooperate with others, how to lead and follow, how to make decisions, and so on.
