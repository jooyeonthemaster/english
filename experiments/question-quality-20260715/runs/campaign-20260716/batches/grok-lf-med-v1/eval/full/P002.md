## 원지문 (변형 전)
There is a tendency, once the dust of an emergency has settled down, to seek the reduction of famine vulnerability primarily in enhanced economic growth, or the revival of the rural economy, or the diversification of economic activities. The potential contribution of greater economic success, if it involves vulnerable groups, cannot be denied. At the same time, it is important to recognize that, no matter how fast they grow, countries where a large part of the population derive their livelihood from uncertain sources cannot hope to prevent famines without specialized entitlement protection mechanisms involving direct public intervention. Rapid growth of the economy in Botswana, or of the agricultural sector in Kenya, or of food production in Zimbabwe, explains at best only a small part of their success in preventing recurrent threats of famine. The real achievements of these countries lie in having provided direct public support to their populations in times of crisis.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
There is a tendency, once the dust of an emergency has settled down, __(A) to seek__ the reduction of famine vulnerability primarily in enhanced economic growth, or the revival of the rural economy, or the diversification of economic activities. The potential contribution of greater economic success, if it involves vulnerable groups, cannot be __(B) denied__. At the same time, it is important to recognize that, no matter how fast they grow, countries __(C) where__ a large part of the population derive their livelihood from uncertain sources cannot hope to prevent famines without specialized entitlement protection mechanisms __(D) involving__ direct public intervention. Rapid growth of the economy in Botswana, or of the agricultural sector in Kenya, or of food production in Zimbabwe, __(E) explaining__ at best only a small part of their success in preventing recurrent threats of famine. The real achievements of these countries lie in having provided direct public support to their populations in times of crisis.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) to seek
(B) denied
(C) where
(D) involving
(E) explaining

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
학생에게 보이는 밑줄 표현 'explaining'은 Rapid growth of the economy in Botswana, or of the agricultural sector in Kenya, or of food production in Zimbabwe라는 or 병렬 NP가 이루는 주절 주어 바로 뒤에 위치한다. 이 긴 주어는 정동사를 반드시 요구하는데 explaining은 현재분사이므로 주절에 정동사가 사라진다. 따라서 정동사 explains로 고쳐야 한다. 병렬 주어가 길어서 분사구문으로 오인하기 쉬운 함정이 있다.
