## 원지문 (변형 전)
We know where we are headed and what we want to do. However, quite often, due to forces outside our control, things do not go as we had planned and we have to adjust to a postponement or create a whole new set of circumstances. This is what happens when life throws us a curveball. The fact is that life is unpredictable. For example, your car breaks down and you are late for an appointment. While it is true that you never arrive at that important meeting, you end up spending a few relaxing hours with people you would never have met otherwise. Remember that not only are curveballs the universe's way of keeping us awake ― which is a gift in and of itself ― but they are also its method of bringing us wonderful surprises.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
We know where we are __(A) headed__ and what we want to do. However, quite often, due to forces outside our control, things do not go as we had planned and we have to adjust to a postponement or __(B) create__ a whole new set of circumstances. This is __(C) what__ happens when life throws us a curveball. The fact is that life is unpredictable. For example, your car breaks down and you are late for an appointment. While it is true that you never arrive at that important meeting, you end up __(D) spending__ a few relaxing hours with people you would never have met otherwise. Remember that not only __(E) being__ curveballs the universe's way of keeping us awake ― which is a gift in and of itself ― but they are also its method of bringing us wonderful surprises.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) headed
(B) create
(C) what
(D) spending
(E) being

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
정답 문장은 Remember that not only being curveballs the universe's way of keeping us awake… 구문이다. that이 이끄는 내포절 안 not only 도치 구조에서 주어 curveballs를 받아 절을 완성할 정동사가 필수인데, 밑줄 표현 being은 준동사(현재분사·동명사)라 서술 기능이 없어 비문이다. 따라서 정동사 are로 고쳐야 한다. 분사구문처럼 읽어 보려 해도 주어-술어 관계가 성립하지 않고 that절 전체가 붕괴하므로 다른 해석이 불가능하다.
