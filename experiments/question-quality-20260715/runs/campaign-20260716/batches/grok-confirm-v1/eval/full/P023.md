## 원지문 (변형 전)
Joseph B. Friedman was an independent American inventor with a broad range of interests and ideas. By the age of fourteen, he had conceptualized his first invention, the lighted pencil, and was attempting to market his idea. Over the course of his inventing career, he would experiment with various ideas. He was issued nine U.S. patents and held patents in Great Britain, Australia and Canada. His first patent was issued for improvements to the fountain pen on April 18, 1922. This was also the first invention that he successfully sold to Sheaffer Pen Company in the mid 1930s. In the 1920s, Friedman began his education in real estate. Although he was working as a real estate agent in San Francisco, California, the 1930s proved to be his most productive patenting period, with six of his nine U.S. patents being issued then. One of these patents was his most successful invention - the flexible drinking straw.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Joseph B. Friedman was an independent American inventor with a broad range of interests and ideas. By the age of fourteen, he had conceptualized his first invention, the lighted pencil, and was attempting to market his idea. Over the course of his inventing career, he would experiment with various ideas. He __(A) was issued__ nine U.S. patents and held patents in Great Britain, Australia and Canada. His first patent was issued for improvements to the fountain pen on April 18, 1922. This was also the first invention __(B) that__ he successfully sold to Sheaffer Pen Company in the mid 1930s. In the 1920s, Friedman began his education in real estate. Although he was __(C) working__ as a real estate agent in San Francisco, California, the 1930s __(D) proving__ to be his most productive patenting period, with six of his nine U.S. patents __(E) being issued__ then. One of these patents was his most successful invention - the flexible drinking straw.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) was issued
(B) that
(C) working
(D) proving
(E) being issued

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
정답 (D)의 보이는 표현은 proving이다. 이 문장은 Although he was working as a real estate agent in San Francisco, California 부사절 다음에 주절 the 1930s ______ to be his most productive patenting period가 오는 구조이다. 주절 주어 the 1930s 뒤에는 반드시 정동사가 와야 문장이 완성되는데 proving은 현재분사(준동사)라서 정동사가 전혀 없어 미완결 비문이 된다. 따라서 proving을 정동사 proved로 고쳐야 한다. 앞 절 was working에 이끌려 주절에도 분사를 허용해도 된다고 착각하는 함정에 주의해야 한다.
