## 원지문 (변형 전)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but spoils its local environment.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
A bridge is normally __(A) constructed__ to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence __(B) making__ a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit __(C) concerns__ how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed __(D) reinforce__ this balance. __(E) Depending__ on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but spoils its local environment.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) constructed
(B) making
(C) concerns
(D) reinforce
(E) Depending

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
Its presence가 뒤따르는 문장의 주어이므로, 뒤에는 문장을 완결해 주는 정동사가 와야 합니다. making은 준동사인 현재분사이므로 단독으로 문장의 정동사 역할을 할 수 없습니다. 따라서 문맥과 시제에 맞춰 준동사인 making을 정동사인 makes로 고쳐야 올바른 문장이 됩니다. 분사구문으로 취급하여도 주절이 없는 불완전한 문장이 되기 때문에 절대로 성립할 수 없습니다.
