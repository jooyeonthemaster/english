## 원지문 (변형 전)
Every year on the second Monday of October, the United States celebrates Columbus Day. This holiday honors Christopher Columbus, the Italian explorer who is credited with discovering America on October 12, 1492. However, considering his mistreatment of Native Americans and the devastating effect of his arrival, honoring the explorer does not make any sense at all. After arriving on the continent, Columbus killed and enslaved thousands of Native Americans. Additionally, the diseases that Columbus and other Europeans brought with them severely reduced the population of Native Americans. Therefore, the entire country should support the creation of a new holiday that honors Native Americans and remembers their suffering. As an alternative to Columbus Day, Americans should celebrate Native American Day or Indigenous Peoples’ Day.

Every year on the second Monday of October, the United States celebrates Columbus Day. This holiday honors Christopher Columbus, the Italian explorer who __(A) is credited__ with __(B) discovering__ America on October 12, 1492. However, __(C) considering__ his mistreatment of Native Americans and the devastating effect of his arrival, honoring the explorer does not make any sense at all. After arriving on the continent, Columbus killed and enslaved thousands of Native Americans. Additionally, the diseases that Columbus and other Europeans brought with them severely __(D) reducing__ the population of Native Americans. Therefore, the entire country should support the creation of a new holiday that honors Native Americans and __(E) remembers__ their suffering. As an alternative to Columbus Day, Americans should celebrate Native American Day or Indigenous Peoples’ Day.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) is credited
(B) discovering
(C) considering
(D) reducing
(E) remembers

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
(D) "reducing"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "reduced"로 고쳐야 한다. 주절 주어 the diseases 뒤에 that 관계절이 붙어 수식하므로 그 자리에 오는 동사는 반드시 정동사 reduced여야 문장이 성립합니다. 관계절 속의 brought를 주절로 오인하더라도 남은 부분이 서술어를 갖지 못하므로 비문임이 확인됩니다. 따라서 올바른 원형 reduced로 고쳐야 합니다.
