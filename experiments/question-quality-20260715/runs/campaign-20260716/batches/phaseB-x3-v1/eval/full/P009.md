## 원지문 (변형 전)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but spoils its local environment.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that __(A) shows__ the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit __(B) concerns__ how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must __(C) either to adapt or__ indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is __(D) subordinate__ to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in __(E) itself__ but spoils its local environment.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) shows
(B) concerns
(C) either to adapt or
(D) subordinate
(E) itself

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
조동사 must 뒤에 걸리는 술어부의 구조적 병렬을 파악하는 문항입니다. 'it must' 뒤에서 조동사 'must'에 연결되는 동사원형 adapt(to)와 (indeed) reinforce가 'either A or B' 구조로 병렬을 이루어야 합니다. 따라서 조동사 바로 뒤에 불필요한 to-부정사 형태를 이끄는 (C) 'either to adapt or'는 비문이며, 조동사 뒤 동사원형의 병렬 형태를 맞춘 'either adapt to or'로 고쳐야 합니다. 동사구 중간에 삽입되어 있는 부사들로 인해 병렬의 짝을 놓치기 쉬운 함정이 있습니다.
