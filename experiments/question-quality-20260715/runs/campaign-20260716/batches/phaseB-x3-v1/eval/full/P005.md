# 문항 P005 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
KILLER

## 유형
GRAMMAR_ERROR

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented them with a list of words and asked them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. What he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented __(A) them__ with a list of words and __(B) asked__ them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the __(C) wrinkled__ face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. __(D) That__ he found was extraordinary. Those students who had __(E) been exposed__ to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) them
(B) asked
(C) wrinkled
(D) That
(E) been exposed

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
문장의 주어 자리에 쓰인 명사절 내에서 타동사 found 뒤에 목적어가 빠져 있는 불완전한 절이 이어지고 있습니다. 따라서 '그가 발견한 것'이라는 대명사 지시 관계을 이끌며 선행사를 자체적으로 포함하는 관계대명사가 필요하므로, 완전한 명사절을 이끄는 접속사인 'That'은 이 자리에 올 수 없으며 'What'으로 고쳐야 합니다.
