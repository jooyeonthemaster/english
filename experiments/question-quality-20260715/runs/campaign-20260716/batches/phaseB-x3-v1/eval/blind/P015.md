## 지문
Carol was new to the United States. One of her friends called to invite her to lunch. She offered to pick her up on the corner of 34th Street and Fifth Avenue at 11:30 a.m. Carol arrived a little early and was standing on the corner waiting for her friend when she noticed a sign above her head. It said, "No Standing." Carol didn't know what to do. She moved away from the sign and started walking back and forth on the street. She was happy to see her friend arrive and couldn't wait to get into her car. She excitedly explained that it was a bad idea to meet at that corner because people aren't allowed to stand there. Her friend said, "What are you talking about? Of course you can stand there." "No," she said, "the sign says 'No Standing'." Her friend burst into laughter. "That means _____."
