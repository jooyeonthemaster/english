We often use the excuse that it is natural to be frustrated when our plans change. That depends, however, on what your priorities are. Is it more important to stick to some rigid work schedule or to be available to my four-year-old? The more general question is, "What's more important - getting what I want and keeping my plans, or learning to go with the flow?" Clearly, to become a more peaceful person, you must prioritize flexibility over rigidity most of the time. I have also found it helpful to expect that a certain percentage of plans will change.
