# 문항 P007

## 지문
According to the individualist form of rhetoric about science, still much used for certain purposes, discoveries are made in laboratories. They are the product of inspired patience, of skilled hands and an inquiring but unbiased mind. Moreover, they speak for themselves, or at least they speak too powerfully and too insistently for prejudiced humans to silence them. It would be wrong to suppose that such beliefs are not sincerely held, yet almost nobody thinks they can provide a basis for action in public contexts. Any scientist who announces a so-called discovery at a press conference without first permitting expert reviewers to examine his or her claims is automatically castigated as a publicity seeker. The norms of scientific communication presuppose that nature does not speak unambiguously, and that _____. A scientific truth has little standing until it becomes a collective product. What happens in somebody’s laboratory is only one stage in its construction.

다음 글의 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은?

## 선택지
① individual discoveries must be driven by personal, unbiased intuition
② scientific breakthroughs gain credibility through immediate public dissemination
③ claims do not attain the status of valid knowledge without collective peer validation
④ scientific validity is determined by how far laboratory findings generate practical utility
⑤ raw experimental data must be replicated flawlessly before any media announcement
