# 문항 P003 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 유형

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Scientists have no special purchase on moral or ethical decisions; a climate scientist is no more qualified to comment on health care reform than a physicist is to judge the causes of bee colony collapse. The very features that create expertise in a specialized domain lead to ignorance in many others. In some cases lay people ― farmers, fishermen, patients, native peoples ― may have relevant experiences that scientists can learn from. Indeed, in recent years, scientists have begun to recognize this: the Arctic Climate Impact Assessment includes observations gathered from local native groups. So our trust needs to be limited, and focused. It needs to be very particular. Blind trust will get us into at least as much trouble as no trust at all. But without some degree of trust in our designated experts ― the men and women who have devoted their lives to sorting out tough questions about the natural world we live in ― we are paralyzed, in effect not knowing whether to make ready for the morning commute or not.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Scientists have no special purchase on moral or ethical decisions; a climate scientist is no more qualified to comment on health care reform than a physicist __(A) is to judge__ the causes of bee colony collapse. The very features that create expertise in a specialized domain __(B) lead__ to ignorance in many others. In some cases lay people ― farmers, fishermen, patients, native peoples ― may have relevant experiences that scientists can learn from. Indeed, in recent years, scientists have begun to recognize this: the Arctic Climate Impact Assessment includes observations __(C) gathered__ from local native groups. So our trust needs to be limited, and focused. It needs to be very particular. Blind trust will get us into at least as much trouble as no trust at all. But without some degree of trust in our designated experts ― the men and women __(D) who__ have devoted their lives to __(E) sort__ out tough questions about the natural world we live in ― we are paralyzed, in effect not knowing whether to make ready for the morning commute or not.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) is to judge
(B) lead
(C) gathered
(D) who
(E) sort

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
밑줄 부분 'sort'는 'devote their lives to sort out' 자리에서 비문이다. 골격은 관계절 'the men and women who have devoted their lives to ~'이고, 동사 devote의 관용 구문 'devote A to B'에서 to는 전치사이므로 목적어로 동명사가 와야 한다. 원형 'sort'를 쓰면 전치사 목적어가 성립하지 않아 문장 구조상 불완전하다. 따라서 동명사 'sorting'으로 고쳐야 한다. to를 부정사 표지로 착각해 원형을 고집하는 함정에 주의해야 한다.
