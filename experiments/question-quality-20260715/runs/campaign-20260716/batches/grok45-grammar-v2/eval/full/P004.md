## 원지문 (변형 전)
There will soon be many self-driving cars with internet access on the road. Although these cars have many benefits, experts warn that having more self-driving cars also means having more potential targets for hackers. Researchers recently proved through a demonstration that even today’s “smart” cars are vulnerable. They used a computer to take control of a car while someone else was driving it. They were not only able to change the radio’s volume, adjust the air conditioner, and switch the windshield wipers on, but they were also able to take control of the transmission and stop the car as it was driving down the highway. Fortunately, it was just a demonstration, but it sends a clear message to both the auto industry and the government: automotive cybersecurity is a real issue that must be taken seriously.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
There will soon be many self-driving cars with internet access on the road. Although these cars have many benefits, experts warn that __(A) having__ more self-driving cars also means having more potential targets for hackers. Researchers recently proved through a demonstration that even today’s “smart” cars __(B) are__ vulnerable. They used a computer to take control of a car while someone else was driving it. They were not only able to change the radio’s volume, __(C) adjust__ the air conditioner, and switch the windshield wipers on, but they were also able to take control of the transmission and stop the car __(D) as__ it was driving down the highway. Fortunately, it was just a demonstration, but it __(E) sending__ a clear message to both the auto industry and the government: automotive cybersecurity is a real issue that must be taken seriously.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) having
(B) are
(C) adjust
(D) as
(E) sending

## 선언된 정답
(E)

## 해설
sending이 놓인 자리의 골격은 접속사 but이 이끄는 절 'it ____ a clear message...'이다. 명시적 주어 it 바로 뒤에 현재분사 sending이 오면 정동사가 사라져 절이 성립하지 않는 비문이 된다. 따라서 sending을 주어 it과 수·시제가 일치하는 정동사 sends로 고쳐야 한다. demonstration 뒤에 이어지는 분사구문처럼 보이기 쉬운 함정이 있으나 but 이후 새 절이 시작된다는 점에 주의해야 한다.
