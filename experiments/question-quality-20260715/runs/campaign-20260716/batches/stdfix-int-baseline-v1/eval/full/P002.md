## 원지문 (변형 전)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but spoils its local environment.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark __(A) that__ shows the evolution of society. It should, then, __(B) be__ an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit __(C) concerning__ how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that __(D) works__ within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in __(E) itself__ but spoils its local environment.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) that
(B) be
(C) concerning
(D) works
(E) itself

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
따라서 문장의 구조를 분석해 보면 'one of the first qualities (a bridge must exhibit)'이 주어로 작용하고 있으며, 이 주어를 서술하는 정동사(본동사) 자리가 필요한데 분사 형태인 (C) 'concerning'이 쓰여 정동사가 없는 비문이 되었습니다. 주어의 핵이 3인칭 단수인 'one'이므로 현재 시제 동사인 'concerns'로 고쳐야 올바른 구조가 됩니다.
