# 문항 P006 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

INTERMEDIATE

## 유형

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Carol was new to the United States. One of her friends called to invite her to lunch. She offered to pick her up on the corner of 34th Street and Fifth Avenue at 11:30 a.m. Carol arrived a little early and was standing on the corner waiting for her friend when she noticed a sign above her head. It said, "No Standing." Carol didn't know what to do. She moved away from the sign and started walking back and forth on the street. She was happy to see her friend arrive and couldn't wait to get into her car. She excitedly explained that it was a bad idea to meet at that corner because people aren't allowed to stand there. Her friend said, "What are you talking about? Of course you can stand there." "No," she said, "the sign says 'No Standing'." Her friend burst into laughter. "That means we cannot park cars there, but we can stop to pick up passengers."

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Carol was new to the United States. One of her friends called to invite her to lunch. She offered to pick her up on the corner of 34th Street and Fifth Avenue at 11:30 a.m. Carol arrived a little early and was standing on the corner waiting for her friend when she noticed a sign above her head. It said, "No Standing." Carol didn't know what to do. She moved away from the sign and started walking back and forth on the street. She was happy to see her friend arrive and couldn't wait to get into her car. She excitedly explained that it was a bad idea to meet at that corner because _____. Her friend said, "What are you talking about? Of course you can stand there." "No," she said, "the sign says 'No Standing'." Her friend burst into laughter. "That means we cannot park cars there, but we can stop to pick up passengers."

## 발문
다음 글의 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은?

## 선택지
① people cannot find the sign there
② cars may stand there as long as they want
③ it is prohibited for pedestrians to stay in this spot
④ pedestrians must stand in a straight line
⑤ everyone should park their cars immediately

## 선언된 정답
③

## 해설
이 글은 미국에 처음 온 캐롤이 'No Standing(주정차 금지)' 표지판을 글자 그대로 '서 있기 금지'로 잘못 이해하여 일어난 유쾌한 오해를 다루고 있습니다. 캐롤은 친구를 만나 그 자리에 서 있으면 안 된다고 다급하게 설명하는 상황이므로, 자신이 오해했던 생각, 즉 '보행자가 그 자리에 머무는 것이 금지되어 있다'는 내용을 말하는 것이 가장 자연스럽습니다. 따라서 원문의 'people aren't allowed to stand there'를 동의어로 패러프레이징한 ③번이 가장 적절합니다. ①, ②, ④, ⑤번은 지문 속 핵심 키워드인 sign, stand, pedestrians, park 등을 부분적으로 조합하거나 사실관계를 뒤틀어 오해의 본질적 인과를 왜곡한 매력적인 오답들입니다.
