# 문항 P001 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
KILLER

## 유형
GRAMMAR_ERROR

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Masks are too heavy to uphold indefinitely, and no matter how well you believe you are disguising yourself, others always know. The consequences are that you waste energy, sacrifice productivity, and lose people's trust. In our study, all of the CEOs were concerned that dropping their masks would make them appear weak. In every case, however, the leader's subordinates perceived the removal of the mask as an act of strength and courage. This, in turn, encouraged a culture where those subordinates did the same, creating a snowball effect. Some of the ways we helped the CEOs remove their masks were to actually name the masks, explore the significant personal and professional costs of upholding them, and ask the CEOs to reflect on the leader they most admired. At this point each of the CEOs was ready to rebuild a leadership identity based on values, strengths, and ambitions.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Masks are too heavy to uphold indefinitely, and no matter how well you believe you are disguising yourself, others always know. The consequences are that you waste energy, sacrifice productivity, and lose people's trust. In our study, all of the CEOs __(A) were concerned__ that dropping their masks would make them __(B) appear__ weak. In every case, however, the leader's subordinates perceived the removal of the mask as an act of strength and courage. This, in turn, encouraged a culture __(C) which__ those subordinates did the same, creating a snowball effect. Some of the ways we helped the CEOs remove their masks were to actually name the masks, explore the significant personal and professional costs of upholding them, and ask the CEOs to reflect on the leader they most admired. At this point each of the CEOs __(D) was__ ready to rebuild a leadership __(E) identity based__ on values, strengths, and ambitions.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) were concerned
(B) appear
(C) which
(D) was
(E) identity based

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
선행사 'a culture'를 수식하는 형용사절에서, 뒤이은 절 'those subordinates did the same'은 주어, 동사, 목적어를 모두 갖춘 완전한 구조입니다. 관계대명사 'which'는 관계절 내에서 주어나 목적어 역할을 수행하며 불완전한 절을 이끌어야 하므로, 이 자리에는 올 수 없습니다. 따라서 장소나 상황의 선행사를 취하고 뒤에 완전한 절을 이끄는 관계부사 'where'로 고쳐야 합니다. 'culture'라는 추상적 개념이 선행사일 때도 관계부사 'where'가 흔히 사용됩니다.
