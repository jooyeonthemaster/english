## 원지문 (변형 전)
Certain species are more crucial to the maintenance of their ecosystem than others. Such species, called keystone species, are vital in determining the nature and structure of the entire ecosystem. The fact that other species depend on or are greatly affected by the keystone species is revealed when the keystone species is removed. It is in this sense that we should draw attention to fig trees. Different species of fig trees may be keystone species in tropical rain forests. Although figs collectively produce a continuous crop of fruits, fruit-eating monkeys, birds, bats, and other vertebrates of the forest do not normally consume large quantities of figs in their diets. During the time of year when other fruits are less plentiful, however, fig trees become important in sustaining fruit-eating vertebrates. Should the fig trees disappear, most of the fruit-eating vertebrates would be eliminated. Protecting fig trees in such tropical rainforest ecosystems is an important conservation goal because it increases the likelihood that monkeys, birds, bats, and other vertebrates will survive.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Certain species are more crucial to the maintenance of their ecosystem than others. Such species, called keystone species, are vital in determining the nature and structure of the entire ecosystem. The fact __(A) that__ other species depend on or are greatly affected by the keystone species __(B) is revealed__ when the keystone species is removed. It is in this sense that we should draw attention to fig trees. Different species of fig trees may be keystone species in tropical rain forests. __(C) Although__ figs collectively produce a continuous crop of fruits, fruit-eating monkeys, birds, bats, and other vertebrates of the forest do not normally consume large quantities of figs in their diets. During the time of year when other fruits are less plentiful, however, fig trees become __(D) importantly__ in sustaining fruit-eating vertebrates. Should the fig trees disappear, most of the fruit-eating vertebrates would be eliminated. Protecting fig trees in such tropical rainforest ecosystems __(E) is__ an important conservation goal because it increases the likelihood that monkeys, birds, bats, and other vertebrates will survive.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) that
(B) is revealed
(C) Although
(D) importantly
(E) is

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
(D) 'importantly'가 포함된 문장의 서술어 구조를 보면, 연결동사 'become'은 주어를 보충 설명하는 주격보어를 필수적으로 요구합니다. 우리말 해석상으로는 '중요하게 된다'로 번역되어 부사인 'importantly'가 자연스러워 보일 수 있으나, 문장 통사 구조상 보어 자리에는 부사가 올 수 없고 형용사만 가능합니다. 따라서 명백하게 틀린 표현인 'importantly'를 형용사형인 'important'로 고쳐야 합니다. 우리말 해석의 함정에 빠져 문장 형식 규칙을 놓치지 않아야 합니다.
