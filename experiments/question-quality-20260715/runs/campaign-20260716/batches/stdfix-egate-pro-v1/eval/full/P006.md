## 원지문 (변형 전)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they are controlled by Denmark, they have their own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber are important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales is not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber is distributed for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law that says each killing must take place as quickly and efficiently as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should be allowed to continue.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
The Faroe Islands are a group of islands in the North Atlantic. Although they are controlled by Denmark, they have __(A) their__ own government. The people of the islands traditionally hunt pilot whales. They have done this for centuries, so whale meat and blubber __(B) is__ important parts of the local diet. The government insists the killing of whales is not done for profit. Most of the whale meat and blubber __(C) is distributed__ for free to the local community, although some is sold in supermarkets. Multiple hunts are held each year under the strict supervision of the local government. There is a law that says each killing must take place __(D) as quickly__ and efficiently as possible. The government claims that the average annual catch is only about 800 whales and that this is a small percentage of the overall pilot whale population. However, environmental groups still question whether this practice should __(E) be allowed__ to continue.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) their
(B) is
(C) is distributed
(D) as quickly
(E) be allowed

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
이 문장의 골격은 'whale meat and blubber'라는 두 명사구가 등위접속사 'and'로 연결되어 복수 주어를 이루고, 이에 호응하는 동사가 오는 구조입니다. 따라서 단수 동사 'is'는 이 자리에 올 수 없으며 복수 형태인 'are'로 고쳐야 합니다. 'so' 뒤에서 새로운 절이 시작되는데, 'whale meat'과 'blubber'가 결합된 형태가 문맥상 단수 취급을 할 만한 하나의 음식 단위를 가리키는 것이 아니라 별개의 두 주요 식습관 요소를 나열하므로 복수 일치가 필수적입니다.
