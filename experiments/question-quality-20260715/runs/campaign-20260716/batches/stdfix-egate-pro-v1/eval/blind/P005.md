## 지문
Animals as well as humans engage in play activities. In animals, play has long __(A) been seen__ as a way of learning and practicing skills and behaviors that are necessary for future survival. In children, too, play has important functions during development. From its earliest beginnings in infancy, play is a way __(B) which__ children learn about the world and __(C) their__ place in it. Children's play serves as a training ground for developing physical abilities - skills like walking, running, and jumping that are necessary for everyday living. Play also allows children __(D) to try__ out and learn social behaviors and __(E) to acquire__ values and personality traits that will be important in adulthood. For example, they learn how to compete and cooperate with others, how to lead and follow, how to make decisions, and so on.
