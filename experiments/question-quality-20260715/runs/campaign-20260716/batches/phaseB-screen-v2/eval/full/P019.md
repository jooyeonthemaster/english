# 문항 P019 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
KILLER

## 유형
GRAMMAR_ERROR

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented them with a list of words and asked them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. What he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates __(A) that__ he was testing their language abilities. He presented them with a list of words and asked them __(B) to create__ a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were __(C) exposed__ to the words. What he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words __(D) taking almost 40 percent longer to walk__ down the hall than __(E) those__ who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것을 고르시오.

## 선택지
(A) that
(B) to create
(C) exposed
(D) taking almost 40 percent longer to walk
(E) those

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
(D) "taking almost 40 percent longer to walk"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "took almost 40 percent longer to walk"로 고쳐야 한다. 주어인 명사구 'Those students' 뒤에 관계대명사절 'who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words'가 수식어로 붙어 있는 구조입니다. 문장의 뼈대를 이루기 위해서는 주어에 호응하는 본동사(정동사)가 반드시 필요하므로 준동사인 (D) 'taking'은 문장을 성립시킬 수 없는 비문입니다. 따라서 과거 시제 정동사인 'took'으로 고쳐야 합니다. (D) 'taking'을 현재분사로 오인하여 주어를 수식하는 구조로 파악하기 쉽지만, 이 경우 전체 주절에 서술어 동사가 사라지게 되는 함정이 있습니다.
