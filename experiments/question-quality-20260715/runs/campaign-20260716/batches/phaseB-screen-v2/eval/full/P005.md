## 원지문 (변형 전)
People typically consider the virtual, or imaginative, nature of cyberspace to be its unique characteristic. Although cyberspace involves imaginary characters and events of a kind and magnitude not seen before, less developed virtual realities have always been integral parts of human life. All forms of art, including cave drawings made by our Stone Age ancestors, involve some kind of virtual reality. In this sense, cyberspace does not offer a totally new dimension to human life. What is new about cyberspace is its interactive nature and this interactivity has made it a psychological reality as well as a social reality. It is a space where real people have actual interactions with other real people, while being able to shape, or even create, their own and other people's personalities. The move from passive imaginary reality to the interactive virtual reality of cyberspace is much more radical than the move from photographs to movies.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
People typically consider the virtual, or imaginative, nature of cyberspace to be its unique characteristic. Although cyberspace __(A) involves__ imaginary characters and events of a kind and magnitude not seen before, less developed virtual realities have always been integral parts of human life. All forms of art, including cave drawings __(B) made__ by our Stone Age ancestors, involve some kind of virtual reality. In this sense, cyberspace __(C) does not offer__ a totally new dimension to human life. What is new about cyberspace is its interactive nature and this interactivity __(D) have made__ it a psychological reality as well as a social reality. It is a space __(E) where__ real people have actual interactions with other real people, while being able to shape, or even create, their own and other people's personalities. The move from passive imaginary reality to the interactive virtual reality of cyberspace is much more radical than the move from photographs to movies.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것을 고르시오.

## 선택지
(A) involves
(B) made
(C) does not offer
(D) have made
(E) where

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
(D) "have made"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "has made"로 고쳐야 한다. 이 글의 중반부 문장 'What is new about cyberspace is its interactive nature and this interactivity has made it...'에서 등위접속사 and가 이끄는 두 번째 절의 주어는 3인칭 단수 명사구인 'this interactivity'입니다. 복수 동사형인 'have made'는 주어의 수와 일치하지 않으므로, 주어의 단수 인칭과 수에 맞추어 단수형 동사인 'has made'로 고쳐야 합니다. 문장 앞부분의 복잡한 절 구조(What이 이끄는 주어절)와 병렬 구조처럼 보이는 착시 때문에 주어를 복수로 오인하기 쉽도록 함정을 판 문항입니다.
