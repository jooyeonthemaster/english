## 원지문 (변형 전)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but spoils its local environment.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a __(A) lasting__ mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge __(B) must exhibit__ __(C) concern__ how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not __(D) acceptable__ to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but __(E) spoils__ its local environment.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것을 고르시오.

## 선택지
(A) lasting
(B) must exhibit
(C) concern
(D) acceptable
(E) spoils

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
문장의 주어부는 'one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit'이며, 이 구조에서 명사구의 핵심어(head)는 복수 명사구의 일원을 나타내는 단수 대명사 'one'입니다. 따라서 술어 동사 자리에는 주어의 수가 단수임을 반영하는 정동사가 와야 하므로, 복수형 동사인 (C) 'concern'은 단수 정동사인 'concerns'로 고쳐야 합니다. 'qualities'나 'exhibit' 또는 복수 명사들에 현혹되어 복수 동사로 수일치 판단을 잘못 내리지 않도록 주의해야 합니다.
