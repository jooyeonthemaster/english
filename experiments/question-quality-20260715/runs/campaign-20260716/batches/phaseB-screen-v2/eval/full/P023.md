## 원지문 (변형 전)
One of the most common mistakes made by organizations when they first consider experimenting with social media is that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program begins not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough understanding of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks because “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of itself. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either solve a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a measurable one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
One of the most common mistakes __(A) made__ by organizations when they first consider experimenting with social media is that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program __(B) begin__ not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough understanding of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks __(C) because__ “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of itself. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either __(D) solve__ a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a __(E) measurable__ one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것을 고르시오.

## 선택지
(A) made
(B) begin
(C) because
(D) solve
(E) measurable

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
이 글의 핵심 주어를 담고 있는 ②번 문장의 구조를 보면, 명사절 접속사 that이 이끄는 보어절 내부의 진짜 주어는 동명사구인 'creating a social media program'입니다. 동명사구 주어는 항상 단수 취급하므로, 이에 호응하는 정동사는 단수 형태인 begins가 되어야 합니다. 따라서 복수 동사 형태인 (B) 'begin'은 어법상 비문이며 단수형인 'begins'로 고쳐야 합니다. 복수 명사인 tools와 channels가 주어 바로 뒤에 길게 이어져 있어 동사의 수일치를 혼동하기 쉽도록 유도한 함정입니다.
