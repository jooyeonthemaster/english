## 원지문 (변형 전)
The ability to understand emotions ― to have a diverse emotion vocabulary and to understand the causes and consequences of emotion ― is particularly relevant in group settings. Individuals who are skilled in this domain are able to express emotions, feelings and moods accurately and thus, may facilitate clear communication between co-workers. Furthermore, they may be more likely to act in ways that accommodate their own needs as well as the needs of others (i.e. cooperate). In a group conflict situation, for example, a member with a strong ability to understand emotion will be able to express how he feels about the problem and why he feels this way. He also should be able to take the perspective of the other group members and understand why they are reacting in a certain manner. Appreciation of differences creates an arena for open communication and promotes constructive conflict resolution and improved group functioning.

Appreciation of differences creates an arena for open communication and _____ and improved group functioning.

## 발문
According to the passage, fill in the blank with the most appropriate option.

## 선택지
① requires individuals to suppress their personal needs
② forces other members to accept one's emotional stance
③ prevents any kind of emotional confrontation
④ fosters productive ways to resolve disagreements
⑤ only focuses on analyzing the causes of negative emotions

## 선언된 정답
④

## 해설
본문은 감정을 이해하는 능력이 모임이나 집단 내에서 원활한 의사소통과 협력을 돕는다고 설명하고 있습니다. 마지막 문장인 'Appreciation of differences creates an arena for open communication and promotes constructive conflict resolution and improved group functioning.'에서 차이를 인정하는 것이 열린 의사소통을 가능하게 하며, '건설적인 갈등 해결(constructive conflict resolution)'을 촉진하고 집단 기능을 향상시킨다고 언급되어 있습니다. 따라서 이 빈칸에 들어갈 정답은 'promotes constructive conflict resolution'을 'fosters productive ways to resolve disagreements'로 패러프레이즈한 것입니다. 이 단서는 앞서 언급된 'In a group conflict situation... a member... will be able to express how he feels'와 연결되어 도출됩니다.
