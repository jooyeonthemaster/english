# 문항 P028

## 지문
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a __(A) lasting__ mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge __(B) must exhibit__ __(C) concern__ how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not __(D) acceptable__ to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but __(E) spoils__ its local environment.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것을 고르시오.

## 선택지
(A) lasting
(B) must exhibit
(C) concern
(D) acceptable
(E) spoils
