# 문항 P011 — 전체 정보 (validity/craft 감사용)

## 요청 난이도
KILLER

## 유형

## 원지문 (변형 전)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented them with a list of words and asked them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the wrinkled face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. What he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words took almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Psychologist John Bargh did an experiment showing human perception and behavior can be influenced by external factors. He told a bunch of healthy undergraduates that he was testing their language abilities. He presented __(A) them__ with a list of words and __(B) asked__ them to create a coherent sentence from it. One of the lists was "DOWN SAT LONELY THE MAN WRINKLED BITTERLY THE WITH FACE OLD". "Bitterly, the lonely old man with the __(C) wrinkled__ face sat down" is one possible solution. But this was no linguistics test. Bargh was interested in how long it took the students to leave the lab and walk down the hall after they were exposed to the words. __(D) That__ he found was extraordinary. Those students who had been exposed to an "elderly" mix of words __(E) took__ almost 40 percent longer to walk down the hall than those who had been exposed to "random" words. Some students even walked with their shoulders bent forwards, dragging their feet as they left, as if they were 50 years older than they actually were.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) them
(B) asked
(C) wrinkled
(D) That
(E) took

## 선언된 정답
(D)

## 해설
주어절을 이끄는 접속사 'That' 뒤에는 빠진 명사가 없는 완전한 절이 와야 합니다. 하지만 이 문장에서 'he found'는 타동사 found의 목적어가 빠진 불완전한 절이므로, 선행사를 포함하면서 대명사 지시 관계을 이끄는 관계대명사 'What'이 필요합니다. 따라서 'That'은 어법상 틀렸으며 복수 명사의 수식을 걷어내고 목적어가 빈 불완전한 절임을 파악하여 'What'으로 고쳐야 합니다. 학생들이 바로 뒤 주어-동사가 온다는 점에만 끌려 접속사 that으로 착각하기 쉬운 고난도 구조입니다.
