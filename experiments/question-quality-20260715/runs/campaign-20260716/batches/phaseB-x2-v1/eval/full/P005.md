## 원지문 (변형 전)
Our emotions are thought to exist because they have contributed to our survival as a species. Fear has helped us avoid dangers, expressing anger helps us scare off threats, and expressing positive emotions helps us bond with others. From an evolutionary perspective, an emotion is a kind of "program" that, when triggered, directs many of our activities (including attention, perception, memory, movement, expressions, etc.). For example, fear makes us very attentive, narrows our perceptual focus to threatening stimuli, will cause us either to face a situation (fight) or avoid it (flight), and may cause us to remember an experience more acutely (so that we avoid the threat in the future). Regardless of the specific ways in which they activate our systems, the specific emotions we possess are thought to exist because they have helped us (as a species) survive challenges within our environment long ago. If they had not helped us adapt and survive, they would not have evolved with us.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
Our emotions are thought to exist because they have contributed to our survival as a species. Fear has helped us __(A) avoid__ dangers, expressing anger helps us scare off threats, and expressing positive emotions helps us bond with others. From an evolutionary perspective, an emotion is a kind of "program" that, when triggered, directs many of our activities (including attention, perception, memory, movement, expressions, etc.). For example, fear makes us very attentive, narrows our perceptual focus to __(B) threatening__ stimuli, will cause us either to face a situation (fight) or avoid it (flight), and may cause us to remember an experience more acutely (so that we avoid the threat in the future). Regardless of the specific ways __(C) which__ they activate our systems, the specific emotions we possess are thought to exist because they have helped us (as a species) survive challenges within our environment long ago. If they __(D) had not helped__ us adapt and survive, they would not have evolved with us.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) avoid
(B) threatening
(C) which
(D) had not helped

## 선언된 정답
(C)

## 해설
이 문장은 'Regardless of' 뒤에 명사구 'the specific ways'가 오고 이를 수식하는 관계사절이 이어지는 구조입니다. (C) 'which' 뒤의 절 'they activate our systems'는 주어, 타동사, 목적어가 모두 갖추어진 완전한 절이므로, 불완전한 절을 이끄는 관계대명사 which는 올 수 없습니다. 따라서 선행사 ways와 결합하여 방법의 부사 역할을 하도록 부사구를 형성하는 전치사 'in'을 동반한 'in which'로 고쳐야 합니다. 선행사가 ways라고 해서 무조건 관계대명사 which를 고르면 뒤의 완전한 구조를 간과하는 실수를 범할 수 있습니다.
