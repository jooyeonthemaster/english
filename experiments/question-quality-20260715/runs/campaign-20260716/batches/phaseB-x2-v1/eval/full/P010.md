## 원지문 (변형 전)
One of the most common mistakes made by organizations when they first consider experimenting with social media is that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program begins not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough understanding of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks because “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of itself. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either solve a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a measurable one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
One of the most common mistakes made by organizations __(A) when__ they first consider experimenting with social media is that they focus too much on social media tools and platforms and not enough on their business objectives. The reality of success in the social web for businesses is that creating a social media program begins not with insight into the latest social media tools and channels but with a thorough __(B) understood__ of the organization’s own goals and objectives. A social media program is not merely the fulfillment of a vague need to manage a “presence” on popular social networks because “everyone else is doing it.” “Being in social media” serves no purpose in and of __(C) itself__. In order to serve any purpose at all, a social media presence must either __(D) solve__ a problem for the organization and its customers or result in an improvement of some sort (preferably a __(E) measurable__ one). In all things, purpose drives success. The world of social media is no different.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) when
(B) understood
(C) itself
(D) solve
(E) measurable

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
(B) "understood"는 어법상 틀린 표현이며 "understanding"로 고쳐야 한다. 이 문장은 전치사구 'with a thorough understanding'을 통해 구조적 뼈대를 이룹니다. 전치사 with 뒤에는 한정사 a와 형용사 thorough의 수식을 받는 명사 핵이 필요하므로, 동작의 의미를 담고 있는 명사형 'understanding'이 와야 합니다. 과거분사 형태인 'understood'는 이 자리에 단독으로 명사구의 핵 역할을 할 수 없어 문장 구조상 비문이 됩니다. 따라서 'understood'를 'understanding'으로 고쳐야 합니다. 형용사적 수식 구조에 현혹되어 분사처럼 잘못 판단하지 않도록 유의해야 합니다.
