## 원지문 (변형 전)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that shows the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed reinforce this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it is subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that works within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in itself but spoils its local environment.

## 렌더된 지문 (학생에게 보이는 형태)
A bridge is normally constructed to last one hundred years in a natural or manmade environment. Its presence makes a lasting mark that __(A) shows__ the evolution of society. It should, then, be an agreeable addition to the local environment. Therefore, one of the first qualities a bridge must exhibit concerns how it harmonizes with its surroundings. It must not upset the balance of the environment; it must either adapt to or indeed __(B) reinforcing__ this balance. Depending on the type of bridge and the site, it __(C) is__ subordinate to the surroundings, or it makes a strong statement. In the latter case the bridge must possess an intrinsic beauty that __(D) works__ within its surroundings. It is not acceptable to create a bridge that is spectacular in __(E) itself__ but spoils its local environment.

## 발문
다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 가장 틀린 것은?

## 선택지
(A) shows
(B) reinforcing
(C) is
(D) works
(E) itself

## 선언된 정답
(B)

## 해설
(B) 조동사 must 뒤에 등위 상관접속사 'either A or B'가 이끄는 동사원형의 병렬 구조를 취해야 하는 자리입니다. 이 문장의 골격은 'it must either [adapt to] or indeed [reinforcing] this balance' 구조이며, 조동사 must의 지배를 받는 첫 번째 동사원형 adapt와 병렬을 이루어야 하므로 동사원형인 reinforce로 고쳐야 합니다. 따라서 준동사 형태인 reinforcing은 어법상 적절하지 않은 표현이 되므로 정답은 (B)가 됩니다. 조동사 뒤 동사원형 병렬이라는 기본 원칙을 잊지 않아야 합니다.
