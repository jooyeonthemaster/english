# Q038

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

We say to ourselves: “There is plenty of time. I’ll manage somehow or other when the time comes for action.” We are rather proud of our ability to meet emergencies. So we do not plan and take precautions __(A) to prevent__ emergencies from arising. It is too easy to drift through school and college, taking the traditional, conventional studies that others take, __(B) following__ the lines of least resistance, electing “snap courses,” and going with the crowd. It is too easy to take the attitude: “First I will get my education and develop myself, and then I will know better __(C) what__ I am fitted to do for a life work.” And so we drift, __(D) driven__ by the winds of circumstance, tossed about by the waves of tradition and custom. Eventually, most men find they must be satisfied with “any port in a storm.” Sailors who select a port because they are driven to it __(E) has__ scarcely one chance in a thousand of dropping anchor in the right one.

## 선택지

(A). to prevent
(B). following
(C). what
(D). driven
(E). has
