# Q048

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

The fast pace of today's lifestyle has us __(A) piling__ one thing on top of another. But you should know that multitasking doesn't save any time. Very often, multitasking only slows you down, contrary __(B) to popular belief__. __(C) Giving__ your whole focus to what you're doing at the moment no matter what it is. While doing the laundry, just do the laundry: Listen to the sound of the water as it fills the washing machine and feel the clothes in your hand. It doesn't take up any more time than it would when __(D) done__ with your phone pressed to your ear. The same applies to your work. Focus on one task at a time, and you'll accomplish each task better, and probably __(E) faster__.

## 선택지

(A). piling
(B). to popular belief
(C). Giving
(D). done
(E). faster
