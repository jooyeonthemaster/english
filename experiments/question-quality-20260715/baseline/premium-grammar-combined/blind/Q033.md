# Q033

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

We have all had the experience of suddenly noticing __(A) that__ a source of constant background noise, such as a distant jackhammer or music from a store, has just ceased - yet we hadn't noticed the sound __(B) while__ it was ongoing. Your auditory areas were predicting its continuation, moment after moment, and as long as the noise __(C) didn't change__ you paid it no attention. __(D) By ceasing__, it violated your prediction and attracted your attention. Here's a historical example. Right after New York City stopped running elevated trains, people called the police in the middle of the night __(E) claimed__ that something woke them up. They tended to call around the time the trains used to run past their apartments.

## 선택지

(A). that
(B). while
(C). didn't change
(D). By ceasing
(E). claimed
