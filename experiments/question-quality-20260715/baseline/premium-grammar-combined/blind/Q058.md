# Q058

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

A god __(A) called__ Moinee was defeated by a rival god called Dromerdeener in a terrible battle up in the stars. Moinee fell out of the stars down to Tasmania to die. Before he died, he wanted to give a last blessing to his final resting place, so he decided __(B) to create__ humans. But he was in such a hurry, knowing he was dying, that he forgot to give them knees; and he absent-mindedly gave them big tails like kangaroos, which __(C) meaning__ they couldn't sit down. Then he died. The people hated __(D) having__ kangaroo tails and no knees, and they cried out to the heavens for help. Dromerdeener heard their cry and came down to Tasmania to see __(E) what__ the matter was. He took pity on the people, gave them bendable knees and cut off their inconvenient kangaroo tails so they could all sit down at last. Then they lived happily ever after.

## 선택지

(A). called
(B). to create
(C). meaning
(D). having
(E). what
