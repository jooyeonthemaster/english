# Q024

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

Unfortunately, many people don't take personal responsibility for their own growth. Instead, they simply run the race __(A) laid__ out for them. They do well enough in school __(B) to keep__ advancing. Maybe they manage to get a good job at a well-run company. But so many think and act __(C) as if__ their learning journey ends with college. They have checked all the boxes in the life that was laid out for them and now lack a road map __(D) described__ the right ways to move forward and continue to grow. In truth, __(E) that__'s when the journey really begins. When school is finished, your growth becomes voluntary. Like healthy eating habits or a regular exercise program, you need to commit to it and devote thought, time, and energy to it. Otherwise, it simply won't happen - and your life and career are likely to stop progressing as a result.

## 선택지

(A). laid
(B). to keep
(C). as if
(D). described
(E). that
