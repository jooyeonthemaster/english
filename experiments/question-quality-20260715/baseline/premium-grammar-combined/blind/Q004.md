# Q004

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

Unfortunately, many people don't take personal responsibility for their own growth. Instead, they simply run the race laid out for them. They do well enough in school to keep __(A) advancing__. Maybe they manage to get a good job at a well-run company. But so many think and act as if their learning journey __(B) ends__ with college. They have checked all the boxes in the life that was laid out for them and now __(C) lacking__ a road map describing the right ways to move forward and continue to grow. In truth, that's when the journey really begins. When school is finished, your growth becomes __(D) voluntary__. Like healthy eating habits or a regular exercise program, you need to commit to it and __(E) devote__ thought, time, and energy to it. Otherwise, it simply won't happen - and your life and career are likely to stop progressing as a result.

## 선택지

(A). advancing
(B). ends
(C). lacking
(D). voluntary
(E). devote
