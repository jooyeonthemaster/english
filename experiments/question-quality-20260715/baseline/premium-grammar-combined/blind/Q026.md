# Q026

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

When I was in the army, my instructors would show up in my barracks room, and the first thing they would inspect __(A) was__ our bed. It was a simple task, but every morning we were required __(B) to make__ our bed to perfection. It seemed a little ridiculous at the time, but the wisdom of this simple act has been __(C) proven__ to me many times over. If you make your bed every morning, you will have accomplished the first task of the day. It will give you a small sense of pride and it will encourage you __(D) doing__ another task and another. By the end of the day, that one task __(E) completed__ will have turned into many tasks completed. If you can't do little things right, you will never do the big things right.

## 선택지

(A). was
(B). to make
(C). proven
(D). doing
(E). completed
