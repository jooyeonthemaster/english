# Q017

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

There is a critical factor that __(A) determines__ whether your choice will influence that of others: the visible consequences of the choice. Take the case of the Adélie penguins. They are often found __(B) strolling__ in large groups toward the edge of the water in search of food. Yet danger awaits in the icy-cold water. There is the leopard seal, for one, which likes to have penguins for a meal. What is an Adélie to do? The penguins' solution is __(C) to play__ the waiting game. They wait and wait and wait by the edge of the water until one of them gives up and jumps in. The moment that occurs, the rest of the penguins __(D) watching__ with anticipation to see what happens next. If the pioneer survives, everyone else will follow suit. If it __(E) perishes__, they'll turn away. One penguin's destiny alters the fate of all the others. Their strategy, you could say, is "learn and live."

## 선택지

(A). determines
(B). strolling
(C). to play
(D). watching
(E). perishes
