# Q023

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

The human brain is wired to look for threats - a trait that __(A) kept__ us alive when we were living on the savannas but that can prevent happiness in our modern lives. This so-called "negativity bias" can keep you __(B) focused__ on what's going wrong (which explains why complaining is such a popular pastime). To break out of this neural rut, train __(C) you__ to acknowledge when things go right. If you keep a calendar or a journal, make a point to write down what went well. If you're more of a verbal processor, start your conversations with friends by __(D) sharing__ a recent win (anything that gives you that yesssss feeling). __(E) Where__ the mind goes, reality follows. The more you appreciate life, the more reasons you have to celebrate it.

## 선택지

(A). kept
(B). focused
(C). you
(D). sharing
(E). Where
