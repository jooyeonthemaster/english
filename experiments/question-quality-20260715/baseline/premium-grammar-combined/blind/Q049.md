# Q049

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

We say to ourselves: “There is plenty of time. I’ll manage somehow or other when the time comes for action.” We are rather proud of our ability __(A) to meet__ emergencies. So we do not plan and take precautions to prevent emergencies from arising. It is too easy to drift through school and college, __(B) taking__ the traditional, conventional studies that others take, following the lines of least resistance, electing “snap courses,” and going with the crowd. It is too easy to take the attitude: “First I will get my education and develop myself, and then I will know better __(C) what__ I am fitted to do for a life work.” And so we drift, driven by the winds of circumstance, tossed about by the waves of tradition and custom. Eventually, most men __(D) finding__ they must be satisfied with “any port in a storm.” Sailors who select a port because they are __(E) driven__ to it have scarcely one chance in a thousand of dropping anchor in the right one.

## 선택지

(A). to meet
(B). taking
(C). what
(D). finding
(E). driven
