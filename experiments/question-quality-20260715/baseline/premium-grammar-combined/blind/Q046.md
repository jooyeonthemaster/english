# Q046

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

Here's an interesting thought. If glaciers started re-forming, they have a great deal more water now to draw on ― Hudson Bay, the Great Lakes, the hundreds of thousands of lakes of Canada, __(A) none of them__ existed to fuel the last ice sheet ― so they would grow very much quicker. And if they did start __(B) to advance__ again, what exactly would we do? Blast them with TNT or maybe nuclear missiles? Well, doubtless we would, but consider this. In 1964, the largest earthquake ever __(C) recorded__ in North America rocked Alaska with 200,000 megatons of concentrated might, the equivalent of 2,000 nuclear bombs. Almost 3,000 miles away in Texas, water __(D) sloshed__ out of swimming pools. A street in Anchorage fell twenty feet. The quake devastated 24,000 square miles of wilderness, much of it __(E) glaciated__. And what effect did all this might have on Alaska's glaciers? None.

## 선택지

(A). none of them
(B). to advance
(C). recorded
(D). sloshed
(E). glaciated
