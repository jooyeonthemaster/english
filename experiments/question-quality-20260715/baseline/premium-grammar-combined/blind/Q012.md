# Q012

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

We all enjoy the pleasure of __(A) being left__ alone with a good book. But sometimes after several hours of immersion we lift our heads and look around, suddenly struck by the quiet, the absence of others. In this situation, we are suffering from reading-induced loneliness. The solution is to read in the company of other people __(B) who__ are reading - whether in a public space such as a café or a library, or in your own home, with your reading friend or partner at the other end of the sofa. Next time you look up, you'll see someone else similarly __(C) immersing__, and you won't feel alone at all. Reading can be sociable. Consider joining a reading group __(D) in which__ everybody takes turns reading aloud from a novel. Reading a book collaboratively __(E) is__ a wonderful way to share an otherwise internal and solitary experience.

## 선택지

(A). being left
(B). who
(C). immersing
(D). in which
(E). is
