# Q059

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

For many years now, mediated entertainment such as TV and film has been able __(A) to stimulate__ our optical and auditory senses with sights and sounds. Some forms of new media, however, even engage our senses of touch and smell. The view the wearer of some special device sees is projected on the screen behind him. Wearers become __(B) immersed__ in the computerized scene and use the gloves to pick up and move simulated objects. Many virtual reality games and rides now allow audiences and players __(C) to feel__ sensations of motion and touch. New media may also include aromas, such as Disney's "Soaring Over California" attraction at the California Adventure theme park, __(D) which__ audiences smell orange orchards and pine forests while enjoying a simulated hang-gliding experience across the countryside. Makers of emerging forms of entertainment will likely continue to experiment with ways __(E) they__ can simulate and manipulate reality by stimulating our senses.

## 선택지

(A). to stimulate
(B). immersed
(C). to feel
(D). which
(E). they
