# Q015

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 적절하지 않은 것은?

The basic guidelines for good style are not mysterious; in fact, you use them every day in conversation. In conversation and in writing, we all rely heavily on cooperation to make sense of exchanges, and a __(a) polished__ practical style makes cooperation easier. Writers develop such a style by acknowledging that readers __(b) expect__ the same things that listeners expect in conversation: clarity, relevance, and proportion. If you listen to someone who is not clear, who cannot stay on the topic, or who offers too much or too little information, you will quickly lose interest in the conversation. Writers, too, need to be clear, stay on the topic, and give information __(c) excessively__. In fact, this attention to audience and appropriateness may be even more important in writing than in conversation because writing does not __(d) permit__ the nonverbal communication and immediate feedback that are part of conversation. As writers, we have to __(e) anticipate__ the absent reader's response; in effect, we have to imagine both halves of a virtual conversation.

## 선택지

1. polished
2. expect
3. excessively
4. permit
5. anticipate
