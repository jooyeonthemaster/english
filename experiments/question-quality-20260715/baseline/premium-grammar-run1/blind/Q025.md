# Q025

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

We tend to think of technology as shiny tools and gadgets. Even if we acknowledge that technology can exist in disembodied form, such as software, we tend not to include in this category paintings, literature, music, dance, poetry, and the arts in general. But we should. If a thousand lines of letters in UNIX __(A) qualifies__ as a technology (the computer code for a web page), then a thousand lines of letters in English (Hamlet) must qualify as well. They both can change our behavior, alter the course of events, or enable future inventions. A Shakespeare sonnet and a Schubert symphony, then, are in the same category as Google's search engine and the smartphone: They are something useful __(B) produced__ by a mind. We can't separate out the multiple overlapping technologies __(C) responsible__ for a Lord of the Rings movie. The literary rendering of the original novel is __(D) as much__ an invention as the digital rendering of its fantastical creatures. Both are useful works of the human imagination. Both __(E) influencing__ audiences powerfully. Both are technological.

## 선택지

(A). qualifies
(B). produced
(C). responsible
(D). as much
(E). influencing
