# Q020

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

We have all had the experience of suddenly __(A) noticing__ that a source of constant background noise, such as a distant jackhammer or music from a store, has just ceased - yet we hadn't noticed the sound __(B) while__ it was ongoing. Your auditory areas were predicting its continuation, moment after moment, and as long as the noise didn't change you paid it no attention. By ceasing, it violated your prediction and __(C) attracted__ your attention. Here's a historical example. Right after New York City stopped __(D) to run__ elevated trains, people called the police in the middle of the night claiming that something woke them up. They tended to call around the time the trains __(E) used to run__ past their apartments.

## 선택지

(A). noticing
(B). while
(C). attracted
(D). to run
(E). used to run
