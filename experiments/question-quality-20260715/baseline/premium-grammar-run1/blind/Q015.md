# Q015

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

Some people need money more than we do. For example, some people have lost their homes __(A) due to__ natural disasters or war, while others don't have enough food or clothing. So this year, for our birthdays, let's tell our friends and family to donate money to a charity instead of __(B) buying__ us presents. I know that some kids might not __(C) wanting__ to give up their birthday presents, and I understand. However, remember that we can live without new toys or games more __(D) easily__ than someone can live without food, clothing, or shelter. So, we should tell our friends and family __(E) that__, for our birthdays this year, we want to give to others.

## 선택지

(A). due to
(B). buying
(C). wanting
(D). easily
(E). that
