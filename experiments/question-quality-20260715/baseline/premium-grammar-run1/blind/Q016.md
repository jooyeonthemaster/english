# Q016

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

A lovely technique for helping children take the first steps towards creating their own, unique story, is to ask __(A) them__ to help you complete a story before you tell it. One story I have done this with frequently is a tale I call Benno and the Beasts. It is based on a story __(B) called__ St. Benno and the Frog, found in an old book by Helen Waddell. In the original, the saint meets a frog in a marsh and tells it __(C) to be__ quiet in case it disturbs his prayers. Later, he regrets this, in case God was enjoying listening to the sound of the frog. I invite children to think of different animals for the saint to meet and different places for him to meet them. I then tell them the story __(D) including__ their own ideas. It is a most effective way of involving children in the art of creating stories and they love hearing their ideas __(E) using__.

## 선택지

(A). them
(B). called
(C). to be
(D). including
(E). using
