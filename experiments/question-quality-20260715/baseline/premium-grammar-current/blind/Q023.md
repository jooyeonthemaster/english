# Q023

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

The fast pace of today's lifestyle has us __(A) piling__ one thing on top of another. But you should know that multitasking doesn't save any time. Very often, multitasking only slows you down, contrary to popular belief. Give your whole focus to __(B) what__ you're doing at the moment no matter what it is. While doing the laundry, just do the laundry: __(C) Listen__ to the sound of the water as it fills the washing machine and feel the clothes in your hand. It doesn't take up any more time than it would when __(D) done__ with your phone pressed to your ear. The same applies to your work. __(E) Focusing__ on one task at a time, and you'll accomplish each task better, and probably faster.

## 선택지

(A). piling
(B). what
(C). Listen
(D). done
(E). Focusing
