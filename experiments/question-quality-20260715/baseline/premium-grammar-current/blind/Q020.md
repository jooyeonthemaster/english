# Q020

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

We all know that tempers are one of the first things __(A) lost__ in many arguments. It's easy to say one should keep cool, but how do you do it? The point to remember is that sometimes in arguments the other person is trying to get you __(B) to be angry__. They may be saying things that are __(C) intentionally__ designed to annoy you. They know that if they get you to lose your cool you'll say something that sounds foolish; you'll simply get angry and then it will be impossible for you to win the argument. So don't fall for it. A remark may be made to cause your anger, but responding with a cool answer that focuses on the issue __(D) raised__ __(E) are__ likely to be most effective. Indeed, any attentive listener will admire the fact that you didn't "rise to the bait."

## 선택지

(A). lost
(B). to be angry
(C). intentionally
(D). raised
(E). are
