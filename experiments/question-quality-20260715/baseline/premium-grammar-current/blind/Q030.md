# Q030

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

Some of the words we use most __(A) frequently__ are not expressive. If someone __(B) asked__ you what you thought of a movie or your dinner, you might respond: "It was fine." Do you mean, 'fine' as in 'fine wine,' or 'fine art?' Or do you mean, 'fine' as in 'excellent, good, fine or poor?' 'Fine' can be __(C) used__ to express satisfaction or disappointment. There are hundreds of words that, like 'fine,' __(D) expresses__ very little meaning. They are fillers. Choose words that are more expressive, like 'great' or 'terrific' or 'wonderful' if you want to express pleasure, and words like 'adequate' or 'satisfactory' if your intent is __(E) to communicate__ that the movie or meal was okay, but nothing more. These words won't be misunderstood.

## 선택지

(A). frequently
(B). asked
(C). used
(D). expresses
(E). to communicate
