# Q015

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

Obviously, one of the judgments the public has of us __(A) is__ whether or not our telephone service is good. Technically, if they get their calls through, efficiently and promptly, they get __(B) what__ they want. That, however, is not all they want. They want to have the service __(C) rendered__ to them in a manner that pleases them; they want not only efficiency but courtesy and consideration; and they are in a position to get what they want. They are in the driver's seat; they are __(D) paying__ the bills; and an understanding of that fact is a real and fundamental basis of public relations. In order, therefore, for a great company __(E) satisfactorily serving__ the public, it must have a philosophy and a method of doing business which will allow and insure that its people serve the public efficiently and in a pleasing manner.

## 선택지

(A). is
(B). what
(C). rendered
(D). paying
(E). satisfactorily serving
