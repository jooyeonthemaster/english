Education is a positional good. Possession of a university qualification indicates an academic achievement that makes
the graduate a more valuable employee. But when university education was accessible only to the social elite,
possession of a university qualification also indicated membership of the elite: it, therefore, also had considerable
positional value. With the mass expansion of higher education following World War II, possession of a university degree

as access to higher education expands, the desire for social differentiation is increasingly sought not just in the fact of
