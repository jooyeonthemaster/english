Three Ways Energy Is Used
The answer to this requires a simple formula and a little bit of math. The formula is an easy equation: the total energy expenditure of the body equals 「BMR + TEF + PA」. In the human body, the energy from different foods people eat is used to keep everything running smoothly. The basal metabolic rate (BMR) is a measurement of how much energy is needed to stay alive; the thermic effect of food (TEF) is a measurement of the energy used to consume and absorb energy from food; and physical activity (PA) is a measurement of the energy used beyond just lying in bed.
