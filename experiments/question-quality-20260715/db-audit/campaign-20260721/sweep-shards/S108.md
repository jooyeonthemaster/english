The idea can be summed up as follows: When setting up laws for a society, you should assume that you could end up in any social position. By doing so, you can avoid being biased by your current situation. Night terrors are unpleasant occurrences that cause sleepers to suddenly be half awake in a state of extreme fear, sometimes screaming or crying. They are not the same as nightmares, which occur during a different stage of sleep. Nightmares usually happen during REM (rapid eye movement) sleep, while night terrors are more likely to occur during NREM (non-rapid eye movement) sleep. Because this type of sleep takes place earlier in the night, night terrors are most common not long after a person has fallen asleep. Nightmares, on the other hand, usually occur after a person has been asleep for quite a while. Another difference is that nightmares are often accompanied by memories of the dream that are quite vivid. People suffering from night terrors, however, do not remember what caused their fear. Experts advise against trying to wake up someone experiencing a night terror. Instead, it is best to sit quietly by their side and to keep them safe by making sure they don’t accidentally harm themselves.
