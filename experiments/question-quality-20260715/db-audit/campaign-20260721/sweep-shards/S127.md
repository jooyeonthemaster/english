Emotional labor refers to the effort required to manage one's feelings or emotions at work according to the emotional
requirements of the situation. Emotional labor involves closing the gap between what you are feeling (e.g..

decisions that would enable them to assess or improve their security. Attempts to solve the fundamental dilemma by

The idea of music direction, in the sense of someone shaping and leading a musical performance, goes back to the beginnings of music.
