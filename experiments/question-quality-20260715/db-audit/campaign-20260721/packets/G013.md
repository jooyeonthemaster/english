When movies are filmed, many sounds are too quiet or unclear for equipment to record them accurately as they take place on set.

To address this issue, special sound-effect artists are required.

Known as Foley artists, these individuals bring movies to life by looking carefully at camera footage and finding items to create sounds that match it.

Being a Foley artist requires a lot of creativity because, despite seeming as though they are authentic, many sounds are replicated in unusual ways.

For instance, the sound of a bird flapping its wings might be produced with a leather wallet.

Ultimately, the work of a Foley artist is invaluable as it adds a sense of authenticity and mood to films that would otherwise have to rely mostly on their visuals.
