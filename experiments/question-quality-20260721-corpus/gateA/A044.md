```

But the role of photographer may actually detract from their delight in the present moment. I know a father who devoted himself earnestly to photographing the birth of his first and only child. The photos were beautiful but, he lamented afterward he felt that he had missed out on the most important first moment of his son's life. Looking through the camera lens made him detached from the scene. He was just an observer, not an experiencer.
