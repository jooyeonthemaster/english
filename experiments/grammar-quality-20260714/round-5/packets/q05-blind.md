# 문항 q05 — INTERMEDIATE (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

We have all had the experience of suddenly noticing that a source of constant background noise, such as a distant jackhammer or music from a store, has just ceased - yet we hadn't noticed the sound __(A) while__ it was ongoing. Your auditory areas were predicting its continuation, moment after moment, and __(B) as long as__ the noise didn't change you paid it no attention. By ceasing, it violated your prediction and attracted your attention. Here's a historical example. Right after New York City stopped running elevated trains, people __(C) called__ the police in the middle of the night __(D) claimed__ that something woke them up. They tended to call around the time the trains used to run past __(E) their__ apartments.
