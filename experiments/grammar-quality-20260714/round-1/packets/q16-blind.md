**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

A lovely technique for __(A) helping__ children take the first steps towards creating their own, unique story, is __(B) to ask__ them to help you complete a story before you tell it. One story I have done this with frequently is a tale I call Benno and the Beasts. It __(C) is based on__ a story called St. Benno and the Frog, found in an old book by Helen Waddell. In the original, the saint meets a frog in a marsh and tells it to be quiet in case it disturbs his prayers. Later, he regrets this, in case God was enjoying listening to the sound of the frog. I invite children to think of different animals for the saint to meet and different places for him to meet __(D) it__. I then tell them the story including their own ideas. It is a most effective way __(E) of involving__ children in the art of creating stories and they love hearing their ideas used.
