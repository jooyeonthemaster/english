# 문항 q07 — INTERMEDIATE (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

We have all had the experience of suddenly noticing that a source of constant background noise, such as a distant jackhammer or music from a store, __(A) has__ just ceased - yet we hadn't noticed the sound __(B) while__ it was ongoing. Your auditory areas were __(C) predicting__ its continuation, moment after moment, and as long as the noise didn't change you paid it no attention. By ceasing, it violated your prediction and attracted your attention. Here's a historical example. Right after New York City stopped __(D) running__ elevated trains, people called the police in the middle of the night __(E) claimed__ that something woke them up. They tended to call around the time the trains used to run past their apartments.
