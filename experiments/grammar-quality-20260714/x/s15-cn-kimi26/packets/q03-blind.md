# 문항 q03 — KILLER (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 밑줄 친 부분 중 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

We tend to think of technology as shiny tools and gadgets. Even if we __(A) acknowledge that__ technology can exist in disembodied form, such as software, we tend not to include in this category paintings, literature, music, dance, poetry, and the arts in general. But we __(B) should__. If a thousand lines of letters in UNIX __(C) qualifies__ as a technology (the computer code for a web page), then a thousand lines of letters in English (Hamlet) must qualify as well. They both can change our behavior, alter the course of events, or enable future inventions. A Shakespeare sonnet and a Schubert symphony, then, are in the same category as Google's search engine and the smartphone: They are something useful produced by a mind. We can't __(D) separate out__ the multiple overlapping technologies responsible for a Lord of the Rings movie. The literary rendering of the original novel is __(E) as much an invention as__ the digital rendering of its fantastical creatures. Both are useful works of the human imagination. Both influence audiences powerfully. Both are technological.
