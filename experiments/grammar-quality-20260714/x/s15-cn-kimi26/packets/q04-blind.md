# 문항 q04 — INTERMEDIATE (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것을 고르시오.

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

The human brain is __(A) wired to look__ for threats - a trait __(B) that kept us alive__ __(C) when we were living__ on the savannas but that can prevent happiness in our modern lives. This so-called "negativity bias" can __(D) keep you focused__ on what's going wrong (which explains why complaining is such a popular pastime). To break out of this neural rut, train yourself to acknowledge when things go right. If you keep a calendar or a journal, __(E) make a point to write__ down what went well. If you're more of a verbal processor, start your conversations with friends by sharing a recent win (anything that gives you that yesssss feeling). Where the mind goes, reality follows. The more you appreciate life, the more reasons you have to celebrate it.
