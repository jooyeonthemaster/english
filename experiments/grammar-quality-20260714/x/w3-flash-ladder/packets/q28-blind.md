**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

Obviously, one of the judgments the public has of us __(A) is__ whether or not our telephone service is good. Technically, if they get their calls through, efficiently and promptly, they get __(B) what__ they want. That, however, is not all they want. They want to have the service rendered to them in a manner that __(C) pleases__ them; they want not only efficiency but courtesy and consideration; and they are in a position to get what they want. They are in the driver's seat; they are __(D) paying__ the bills; and an understanding of that fact is a real and fundamental basis of public relations. In order, therefore, for a great company to __(E) satisfactory__ serve the public, it must have a philosophy and a method of doing business which will allow and insure that its people serve the public efficiently and in a pleasing manner.
