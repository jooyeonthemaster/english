# 문항 q19 — INTERMEDIATE (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

The whole of human society operates on __(A) knowing__ the future weather. For example, farmers in India know when the monsoon rains will come next year and so they know when __(B) planting__ the crops. Farmers in Indonesia know there are two monsoon rains each year, so next year they can have two harvests. This is __(C) based__ on their knowledge of the past, as the monsoons have always come at about the same time each year in living memory. But the need to predict goes deeper than this; it influences every part of our lives. Our houses, roads, railways, airports, offices, and so on __(D) are all designed__ for the local climate. For example, in England all the houses have central heating, as the outside temperature is usually __(E) below__ 20°C, but no air-conditioning, as temperatures rarely go beyond 26°C, while in Australia the opposite is true: most houses have air-conditioning but rarely central heating.
