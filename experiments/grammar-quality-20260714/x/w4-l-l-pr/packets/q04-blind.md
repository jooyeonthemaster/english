# 문항 q04 — INTERMEDIATE (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

The human brain is __(A) wired__ to look for threats - a trait that kept us alive when we were living on the savannas but that can prevent happiness in our modern lives. This so-called "negativity bias" can keep you focused on what's going wrong (which explains why complaining is such a popular pastime). To break out of this neural rut, train __(B) yourself__ to acknowledge when things go right. If you keep a calendar or a journal, make a point to write down __(C) that__ went well. If you're more of a verbal processor, start your conversations with friends by __(D) sharing__ a recent win (anything that gives you that yesssss feeling). Where the mind goes, reality follows. __(E) The more__ you appreciate life, the more reasons you have to celebrate it.
