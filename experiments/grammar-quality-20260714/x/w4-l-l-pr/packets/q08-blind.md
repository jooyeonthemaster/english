**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

The fast pace of today's lifestyle has us __(A) piling__ one thing on top of another. But you should know that multitasking doesn't save any time. Very often, multitasking only slows you down, contrary to popular belief. Give your whole focus to __(B) what__ you're doing at the moment no matter what it is. While __(C) doing__ the laundry, just do the laundry: Listen to the sound of the water as it fills the washing machine and feel the clothes in your hand. It doesn't take up any more time than it would when done with your phone __(D) pressed__ to your ear. The same __(E) is applied__ to your work. Focus on one task at a time, and you'll accomplish each task better, and probably faster.
