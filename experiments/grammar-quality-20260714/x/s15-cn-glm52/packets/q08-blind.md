**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 (A)~(E) 중, 어법상 **틀린 것**은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

The fast pace of today's lifestyle __(A) has us piling__ one thing on top of another. But you should know that multitasking doesn't save any time. Very often, multitasking only slows you down, __(B) contrary to popular belief__. Give your whole focus to what you're doing at the moment no matter what it is. While doing the laundry, just do the laundry: Listen to the sound of the water as it fills the washing machine and feel the clothes in your hand. It doesn't take up any more time than it would __(C) when done__ with __(D) your phone pressed__ to your ear. The same applies to your work. Focus on one task at a time, and you'll accomplish each task better, and probably faster.
