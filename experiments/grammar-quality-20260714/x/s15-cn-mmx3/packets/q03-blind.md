**발문**: Below is a passage with five underlined parts (A) through (E). Choose the one that contains a grammatical error.

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

We tend to think of technology as shiny tools and gadgets. Even if we acknowledge that technology can exist in disembodied form, such as software, we __(A) tend not to include in this category paintings, literature, music, dance, poetry, and the arts in general__. But we should. __(B) If a thousand lines of letters in UNIX qualifies as a technology__ (the computer code for a web page), then a thousand lines of letters in English (Hamlet) must qualify as well. __(C) They both can change our behavior, alter the course of events, or enable future inventions__. A Shakespeare sonnet and a Schubert symphony, then, are in the same category as Google's search engine and the smartphone: __(D) They are something useful producing by a mind__. We can't separate out the multiple overlapping technologies responsible for a Lord of the Rings movie. __(E) The literary rendering of the original novel is as much an invention as the digital rendering of its fantastical creatures__. Both are useful works of the human imagination. Both influence audiences powerfully. Both are technological.
