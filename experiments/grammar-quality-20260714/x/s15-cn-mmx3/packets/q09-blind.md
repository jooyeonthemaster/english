# 문항 q09 — KILLER (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 옳지 않은 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

There is a critical factor that determines whether your choice will influence that of others: the visible consequences of the choice. Take the case of the Adélie penguins. __(A) They are often found strolling in large groups toward the edge of the water in search of food.__ Yet danger awaits in the icy-cold water. There is the leopard seal, for one, which likes to have penguins for a meal. What is an Adélie to do? __(B) The penguins' solution is to play the waiting game.__ They wait and wait and wait by the edge of the water __(C) until one of them gives up and jumps in.__ __(D) The moment occurs, the rest of the penguins watch with anticipation to see what happens next.__ __(E) If the pioneer survives, everyone else will follow suit.__ If it perishes, they'll turn away. One penguin's destiny alters the fate of all the others. Their strategy, you could say, is "learn and live."
