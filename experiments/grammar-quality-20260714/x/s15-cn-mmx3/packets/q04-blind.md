# 문항 q04 — INTERMEDIATE (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 밑줄 (A)~(E) 중 어법상 옳지 않은 것 하나를 고르시오.

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

The human brain is wired to look for threats - __(A) a trait that kept us alive__ when we were living on the savannas but that can prevent happiness in our modern lives. This so-called "negativity bias" can keep you focused on what's going wrong (which explains why complaining is such a popular pastime). To break out of this neural rut, train yourself __(B) to acknowledge when things go right__. If you keep a calendar or a journal, __(C) make a point to write down__ what went well. If you're more of a verbal processor, start your conversations with friends by sharing a recent win (__(D) anything that gives you that yesssss feeling__). Where the mind goes, reality follows. The more you appreciate life, the more reasons you have __(E) for celebrate it__.
