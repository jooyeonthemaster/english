# 문항 q15 — KILLER (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

Suppose that you are busy working on a project one day and you have no time __(A) to buy__ lunch. All of a sudden your best friend shows up with your favorite sandwich. He tells you that he knows you are busy and he wants to help you out by __(B) buying__ you the sandwich. In this case, you are very likely to appreciate your friend's help. However, if a stranger __(C) showing__ up with the same sandwich and offers it to you, you won't appreciate it. Instead, you would be confused. You would likely think "Who are you, and how do you know __(D) what__ kind of sandwich I like to eat?" The key difference between these two cases is the level of trust. You trust your best friend so much that you won't worry about him __(E) knowing__ you too well, but you certainly would not give the same level of trust to a stranger.
