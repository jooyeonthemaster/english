**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 (A)~(E) 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

We __(A) have all had__ the experience of suddenly noticing that a source of constant background noise, such as a distant jackhammer or music from a store, has just ceased - yet we hadn't noticed the sound while it was ongoing. Your auditory areas __(B) were predicting__ its continuation, moment after moment, and as long as the noise didn't change you paid it no attention. __(C) By to cease__, it violated your prediction and attracted your attention. Here's a historical example. Right after New York City __(D) stopped running__ elevated trains, people called the police in the middle of the night claiming that something woke them up. They tended to call around the time the trains __(E) used to run__ past their apartments.
