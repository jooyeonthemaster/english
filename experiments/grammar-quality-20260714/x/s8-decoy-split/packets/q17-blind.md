# 문항 q17 — ADVANCED (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

A lovely technique for helping children take the first steps towards creating their own, unique story, is to ask them to __(A) help__ you complete a story before you tell it. One story I have done this with frequently is a tale I call Benno and the Beasts. It is based on a story called St. Benno and the Frog, __(B) found__ in an old book by Helen Waddell. In the original, the saint meets a frog in a marsh and tells it to be quiet in case it disturbs his prayers. Later, he regrets this, in case God was enjoying __(C) to listen__ to the sound of the frog. I invite children to think of different animals for the saint __(D) to meet__ and different places for him to meet them. I then tell them the story including their own ideas. It is a most effective way of involving children in the art of creating stories and they love hearing their ideas __(E) used__.
