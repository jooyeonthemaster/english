# 문항 q09 — KILLER (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 (A)~(E) 중 어법상 가장 부적절한 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

__(A) There is a critical factor that determines whether your choice will influence that of others: the visible consequences of the choice.__ Take the case of the Adélie penguins. __(B) They are often found strolling in large groups toward the edge of the water in search of food.__ Yet danger awaits in the icy-cold water. __(C) There is the leopard seal, for one, which likes to have penguins for a meal.__ What is an Adélie to do? The penguins' solution is to play the waiting game. They wait and wait and wait by the edge of the water until one of them gives up and jumps in. __(D) The moment that occur, the rest of the penguins watch with anticipation to see what happens next.__ If the pioneer survives, everyone else will follow suit. If it perishes, they'll turn away. __(E) One penguin's destiny alters the fate of all the others.__ Their strategy, you could say, is "learn and live."
