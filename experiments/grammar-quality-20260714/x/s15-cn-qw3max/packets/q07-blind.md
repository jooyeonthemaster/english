# 문항 q07 — INTERMEDIATE (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: **다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중 어법상 가장 부적절한 것은?**

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

We have all had the experience of suddenly noticing that a source of constant background noise, such as a distant jackhammer or music from a store, __(A) has just ceased__ - yet __(B) we hadn't noticed__ the sound while it was ongoing. __(C) Your auditory areas were predicting__ its continuation, moment after moment, and as long as the noise didn't change __(D) you paid it no attention__. By ceasing, it violated your prediction and attracted your attention. Here's a historical example. Right after New York City stopped running elevated trains, __(E) people called the police__ in the middle of the night claiming that something woke them up. They tended to call around the time the trains used to run past their apartments.
