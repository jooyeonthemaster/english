# 문항 q02 — ADVANCED (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중 어법상 옳지 않은 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

__(A) We all enjoy the pleasure of being left alone with a good book.__ But sometimes after several hours of immersion we lift our heads and look around, suddenly struck by the quiet, the absence of others. __(B) In this situation, we are suffering from reading-induced loneliness.__ __(C) The solution is to read in the company of other people who are reading__ - whether in a public space such as a café or a library, or in your own home, with __(D) your reading friend or partner on the other end of the sofa__. Next time you look up, you'll see someone else similarly immersed, and you won't feel alone at all. Reading can be sociable. Consider joining a reading group in which everybody takes turns reading aloud from a novel. __(E) Reading a book collaboratively is a wonderful way to share an otherwise internal and solitary experience.__
