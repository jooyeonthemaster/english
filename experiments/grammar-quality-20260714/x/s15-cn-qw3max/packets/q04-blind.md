# 문항 q04 — INTERMEDIATE (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중 어법상 가장 부적절한 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

The human brain is wired to look for threats - a trait that kept us alive when we were __(A) living on the savannas__ but that can prevent happiness in our modern lives. This so-called "negativity bias" can keep you focused on __(B) what's going wrong__ (which explains why complaining is such a popular pastime). To break out of this neural rut, __(C) train yourself acknowledging when things go right__. If you keep a calendar or a journal, __(D) make a point to write down what went well__. If you're more of a verbal processor, start your conversations with friends by sharing a recent win (__(E) anything that gives you that yesssss feeling__). Where the mind goes, reality follows. The more you appreciate life, the more reasons you have to celebrate it.
