# 문항 q21 — INTERMEDIATE (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

Some of the words we use __(A) most__ frequently are not expressive. If someone asked you __(B) what__ you thought of a movie or your dinner, you might respond: "It was fine." Do you mean, 'fine' as in 'fine wine,' or 'fine art?' Or do you mean, 'fine' as in 'excellent, good, fine or poor?' 'Fine' can __(C) be used__ to express satisfaction or disappointment. There are hundreds of words that, like 'fine,' __(D) expresses__ very little meaning. __(E) They__ are fillers. Choose words that are more expressive, like 'great' or 'terrific' or 'wonderful' if you want to express pleasure, and words like 'adequate' or 'satisfactory' if your intent is to communicate that the movie or meal was okay, but nothing more. These words won't be misunderstood.
