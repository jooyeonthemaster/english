Before he died, he wanted to give a last blessing to his final resting place, __(B) so__ he decided to create humans. But he was in such a hurry, __(C) knowing__ he was dying, that he forgot to give them knees; and he absent-mindedly gave them big tails like kangaroos, __(D) which__ meant they couldn't sit down. Then he died. The people hated having kangaroo tails and no knees, and they cried out to the heavens for help. Dromerdeener heard their cry and came down to Tasmania to see what the matter was. He took pity on the people, gave them bendable knees and __(E) cutting__ off their inconvenient kangaroo tails so they could all sit down at last. Then they lived happily ever after.
