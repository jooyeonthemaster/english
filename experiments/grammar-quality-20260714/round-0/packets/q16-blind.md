**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

Suppose that you are busy working on a project one day and you have no time to __(A) buy__ lunch. All of a sudden your best friend shows up with your favorite sandwich. He tells you that he knows you are busy and he __(B) wanting__ to help you out by buying you the sandwich. In this case, you are very likely to appreciate your friend's help. However, if a stranger shows up with the same sandwich and offers it to you, you won't appreciate it. Instead, you would be confused. You would likely think "Who are you, and how do you know __(C) what__ kind of sandwich I like to eat?" The key difference between these two cases __(D) is__ the level of trust. You trust your best friend so much that you won't worry about him knowing you too well, but you certainly would not give the same level of trust __(E) to__ a stranger.
