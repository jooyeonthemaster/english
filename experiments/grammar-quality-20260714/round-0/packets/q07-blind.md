# 문항 q07 — ADVANCED (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

The fast pace of today's lifestyle __(A) has__ us piling one thing on top of another. But you should know __(B) that__ multitasking doesn't save any time. Very often, multitasking only slows you down, contrary to popular belief. Give your whole focus to what you're doing at the moment no matter what it is. __(C) While doing__ the laundry, just do the laundry: Listen to the sound of the water as it fills the washing machine and feel the clothes in your hand. It doesn't take up any more time than it would when done with your phone __(D) pressed__ to your ear. The same applies to your work. __(E) Focusing__ on one task at a time, and you'll accomplish each task better, and probably faster.
