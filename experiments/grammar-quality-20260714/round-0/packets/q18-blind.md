# 문항 q18 — INTERMEDIATE (블라인드: 먼저 직접 푸시오)

**발문**: 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

**지문(밑줄 = (A)~(E) 마커 뒤 표현)**:

Many people take the commonsense view __(A) that__ color is an objective property of things, or of the light that bounces off __(B) them__. They say a tree's leaves are green because they reflect green light - a greenness that is just as real as the leaves. Others argue that color doesn't inhabit the physical world at all but exists only in the eye or mind of the viewer. They maintain that if a tree fell in a forest and no one was there to __(C) see__ it, its leaves __(D) did__ be colorless - and so would everything else. They say there is no such thing as color; there are only the people __(E) who__ see it. Both positions are, in a way, correct. Color is objective and subjective - "the place," as Paul Cézanne put it, "where our brain and the universe meet." Color is created when light from the world is registered by the eyes and interpreted by the brain.
